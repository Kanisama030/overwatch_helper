# Overwatch Overwatch Tracer Guide and Overview

In this Overwatch Tracer guide, you'll learn the fundamentals of their abilities, unique play styles, and fundamental tips. Master ability combos, positioning, and matchup knowledge to excel with other Overwatch [heroes](https://mobalytics.gg/overwatch/heroes/tracer-guide/overwatch/heroes)!

## Tracer Overview

The original face of Overwatch, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/tracer.png)Tracer has been one of the most game-defining heroes ever introduced. Zipping around fights, bursting down Supports, and getting multi-kills with a nice ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/pulse-bomb.png)Pulse Bomb stick have made her a crowd favorite. Her kit and design are so good, the only time she’s ever fallen out of a top meta position is when her damage gets nerfed into the ground. 

She can keep up with every dive hero and in coordinated attacks, she deletes backlines. Even when she’s not getting solo kills, just her presence poses a huge threat and interrupts the enemy team’s game plan. Most of her time is spent behind enemy lines, surviving on her own and looking for targets she can isolate. 

### TLDR

#### Play As

* Dive the backline. The number one job of every Tracer is to either kill or distract the enemy backline. Even if the Supports live, they can’t heal the rest of their team if they're trying to fight you.
* Avoid Tanks. Most Tanks have armor, which negates a lot of your damage anyway. Tracer can annoy Tanks, but she’s wasting her time if she is focusing on them.
* Don’t go in without ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/recall.png)Recall or two ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/blink.png)Blinks. Tracer can’t survive fights without the ability to dodge and rewind time. Waiting a few extra seconds for your cooldowns is far better than going in and getting sent back to spawn.

#### Play Against

* Keep your distance. Tracer has terrible ranged damage so try to position where it's difficult for her to reach you, or use mobility tools to dodge her.
* Track ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/recall.png)Recall. You can get used to the timing of Recall to predict where she will rewind to. If you see Recall used, that is your chance to chase and eliminate Tracer.
* If you see Tracer multi-Blink straight at you, expect a ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/pulse-bomb.png)Pulse Bomb. Use a barrier, stun, or escape tool to avoid getting stuck.

## Strengths And Weaknesses Explained

### Strengths

#### Self-reliant

Tracer can heal herself and use her mobility to grab health packs when needed. At very close ranges, she can one-clip enemy backlines without having to coordinate a dive attack. Unlike most heroes, she can pretty much ignore what her team is doing and execute her own game plan.

#### Evasive and mobile

Her small body and fast movement make her difficult to hit. She can reliably dodge enemy cooldowns and Ults with Blinks and Recall. She’s also one of the best overtime stallers in the DPS category because she gets back to point quickly, then touches, evades, and touches again to keep resetting the OT timer. 

#### Nuisance

The hardest part about dealing with a skilled Tracer is that she is always being a nuisance. You can never fully ignore her, but it often feels impossible to kill her. She can single-handedly keep both enemy Supports preoccupied, while her team kills everyone else.

### Weaknesses

#### Short range only

The main downside of Tracer's kit is the limited effective range of Pulse Pistols. Unless you can get close to your target, your damage will barely tickle the enemy. Wide bullet spread and harsh damage falloff prevent Tracer from dealing meaningful damage at medium to long ranges.

#### Mechanically demanding

One reason Tracer has always been a staple in pro and top 500 meta, even when she’s been weak in regular ranked play, is that she has a high skill floor and ceiling. Tracking and Blink mechanics make or break good Tracer mains. Even if you execute a perfect strategy, it won’t matter if you can’t land your shots, get lost while Blinking, or whiff Pulse Bomb.

#### Low health

Winning the award for lowest health of any hero, Tracer relies on movement to stay alive. She gets insta-killed by a lot of attacks and combos that every other hero would survive. You have to play extra carefully when using Tracer, as small amounts of spam damage quickly deplete her small health pool.

## Tracer Ability Tips

### Primary Fire: Pulse Pistols

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/pulse-pistols.png)Pulse Pistols  

* Stay close
* * Tracer’s ideal range is about 7m or closer. From this distance, the majority of bullets stay within an enemy's hurtbox, and the damage falloff doesn’t kick in.  
   * The closer you are, the more consistently you’ll land all bullets. You’ll also be able to go for headshots at very close distance and benefit from the double damage.
* Poke
* * A bit of poke damage is better than nothing. However, your time will usually be better spent setting up flanks.  
   * Any damage resets the universal self-healing passive. So, against Supports shooting just to get in some damage and stop their self-healing can buy you extra time to keep their health low while you close the distance between you.

### \[SHIFT\]: Blink

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/blink.png)Blink  

* Dodge
* * Anytime you are attacking or dueling, Blinks are your primary means of staying alive. Change up your Blink movement with left and right Blinks, crossup Blinks, and Blinking behind corners.
* Traverse the map
* * You should always use Blinks to get around faster, until you are getting near the enemy team. Don’t want to get caught with no Blinks and then have to immediately use Recall just to stay alive.
* Engage and disengage
* * Blinks help you get into your ideal range faster and safer, so you can start attacking. Be careful never to use all three to get into position.  
   * Always save at least one Blink to dodge.  
   * If you don’t have Recall or want to save Recall, Blinks are a great way to back out of a fight, reassess the situation then go back in.

### \[E\]: Recall

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/recall.png)Recall  

* Use as late as possible
* * Focus on using Blinks to survive and only Recalling when absolutely necessary.  
   * Waiting until you’ve taken more damage also helps to get more enemy resources out before you get to reset the fight.  
   * Just don’t activate too late, as you can be killed or stunned during its brief startup animation.
* Never die with Recall
* * Most importantly, better safe than sorry. If you die with Recall, 100%, you know you wasted a death and the long trip back from spawn.
* Play safe
* * Keep your distance when Recall is on cooldown. Don’t overcommit when you have no escape.

### Ultimate: Pulse Bomb

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/pulse-bomb.png)Pulse Bomb  

* Sneak attack
* * Sticking a target is difficult. To improve your odds, always try to sneak up on people so they won’t be trying to dodge you.  
   * Wait behind walls, or come from behind and make sure they aren’t looking at you.
* Track counter abilities
* * Heroes who have abilities like: Moira Fade, Zarya bubble, Cassidy Roll, Kiriko Cleanse, etc., can use them to survive a Pulse Bomb stick.  
   * Always wait until you see these abilities used before attempting to use Pulse Bomb.

## Perks

### Minor Perks

Chronal Dash  

* Increased movement, and can be good for chasing enemies with high mobillity.
* The main problem is that you would have to adjust muscle memory of blink distance mid-round.
* Not worth taking over Kinetic Reload.

Kinetic Reload  

* Recommended Minor Perk
* Incredibly strong Perk to help secure elims. You literally restore 50% of your ammo with each Melee.
* Blink > Melee when you need just a few more bullets for the kill, as quick melee is faster than reloading.

### Major Perks

Blink Packs  

* Considering ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/tracer.png)Tracer uses frequently grabs health packs, this will help you get back into the fight a bit faster, multiple times per game.
* Great when dueling and are able to grab a health pack. Tracer puts herself in a lot of 1v1 scenarios. Health packs restoring a Blink give her increased survivability.
* * Also, she can now steal health packs from the enemy **even when she’s at full health**. Don’t let them grab it!

#### Quantum Entanglement  

* Helpful for confirming elims
* * Sometimes, enemies survive with a bit of health. Overhealth and extra ammo come in handy to help stay in the fight after Recalling to get that final blow.
* Should probably still disengage
* * Even with the overhealth, if you have no Blinks available, you should still get out of the fight and wait for your cooldowns to refresh.  
   * In General, don't go aggro after using ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/recall.png)Recall.

## Maps

Tracer’s mobility allows her to find value on virtually every map. The best maps for Tracer, however, are flat maps with plenty of side rooms for flanking. When Tracer can abuse corners and side routes with health packs, her job of being a backline assassin becomes considerably easier. Because she lacks vertical mobility, she can have trouble on maps with lots of high ground that the enemy can use to avoid her. On wide open maps with long sight lines, Tracer wastes a lot of time pathing to avoid enemies with superior range.

### Best Maps

* New Junk City
* Oasis
* Hanaoka

### Worst Maps

* Junkertown
* Circuit Royal
* Midtown

## Team Comp Synergies

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/tracer.png)Tracer can do everything she's meant to do all on her own. She doesn't need a specific team comp to function. That said, however, dive comps are where she can shine the brightest.

A coordinated dive with another DPS or Tank makes her flanks extremely lethal. Also, having another hero in the enemy backlines with her takes attention off of her and makes her actions safer. Dive Tanks like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/winston.png)Winston or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/d-va.png)D.Va, and dive DPS like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/genji.png)Genji or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/vendetta.png)Vendetta, are good choices as they and Tracer can keep up with each other's mobility.

Passive Support healing like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/orb-of-harmony.png)Orb of Harmony and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/restorative-stream.png)Restorative Stream are really strong for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/tracer.png)Tracer since she can zip around while healing. Her HP is very small, so the light healing brings higher value on her than the huge HP of a Tank.

## How to Counter Tracer 

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/tracer.png)Tracer can win games by being Pro-status and going in and deleting everyone one by one. Most of the time for the "mortal" gamers, she wins by securing occasional assassinations and being a constant nuisance. You'll need to be able to stop her from harassing your Supports if you want to win.

The best way to do so is with Stuns like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/mizuki.png)Mizuki’s ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/binding-chain.png)Binding Chain, or protection like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/torbjorn.png)Torbjörn’s ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/deploy-turret.png)Deploy Turret.

Keep your distance. Tracer has terrible ranged damage, so try to position where it's difficult for her to reach you or use mobility tools to dodge her.

Track Recall, and if you haven't seen her use it, save your offensive cooldowns until after. You can get used to the timing of Recall to predict where she will rewind to. If you see Recall used, that is your chance to chase and eliminate Tracer.

If you see Tracer multi-![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/blink.png)Blink straight at you, expect a ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/pulse-bomb.png)Pulse Bomb.
