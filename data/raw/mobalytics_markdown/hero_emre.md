# Overwatch Overwatch Emre Guide and Overview

In this Overwatch Emre guide, you'll learn the fundamentals of their abilities, unique play styles, and fundamental tips. Master ability combos, positioning, and matchup knowledge to excel with other Overwatch [heroes](https://mobalytics.gg/overwatch/heroes/emre-guide/overwatch/heroes)!

## Emre Overview

A former principled Overwatch agent who left as the organization lost its way. He was hunted down by bounty hunter Freja, who found him heavily modified with cybernetics. His mind is at war with itself, toggling between his old self and a soulless killing machine.

Emre uses his Burst Rifle take over the ranged battle. While most other heroes sacrifice either accuracy or movement speed, Emre maintains both as he ADS strafes to be simultaneously lethal and evasive.

Taking off angles helps him to bypass barrier Tanks, find targets behind cover. When he catches someone off guard, he can get them with a direct nade and finish them off with a rifle burst. He also has great self-sustain thanks to life steal, and his health regen passive.

### TLDR

#### Play As

* ADS as much as possible. Only unscope when there is no target, or when fighting at very close range.
* Keep yourself alive and don’t rely on Support healing. Use Siphon Blaster when you need healing.
* Throw Cyber Frag and quickly try to shoot the same target. Frag + headshot is his most lethal combo.

#### Play As

* If you don’t have good burst damage, try to escape during his Siphon Blaster, so you minimize how much life steal he can generate.
* Emre has no crowd control counters other than trying to dodge with Siphon Blasters. He’s an easy kill if you can stun or knock him around.
* Listen for his Ult voice line, and prepare to shoot Emre out of the sky.

## Strengths And Weaknesses Summarized

* Good at medium to long-range
* Fast Movement
* Self-sustain

* Vulnerable during Ult
* Damage output is low
* Weaker version of other DPS

## Strengths And Weaknesses Explained

### Strengths

#### **Good Medium to Long-Range Dueling**

Being able to hip fire and ADS his assault rifle gives him a decent amount of versatility in the ranges at which he is effective. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/take-aim-emre.png)Take Aim (Emre) makes him very strong in medium to long-range duels.

#### **Fast Movement**

While he is not moving at the hyperspeed of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/jump-pack.png)Jump Pack, Emre has the best ADS strafe speed in the game, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/siphon-blaster.png)Siphon Blaster gives him both vertical and horizontal mobility. He never has to sit still, making him a difficult target for the enemy team.

#### **Self-Sustain**

Self-sustain is a rare commodity in the DPS roster. Although the lifesteal from ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/siphon-blaster.png)Siphon Blaster is not an insane amount of burst healing, it can definitely keep Emre alive in 1v1s. Having movement paired with lifesteal makes Emre more self-sufficient and less reliant on team assistance.

### **Weaknesses**

#### **Vulnerable During Ultimate**

Floating around in the air during his ult makes him an easy target for enemy hitscans. Expect any enemy with anti-air capabilities to focus on taking him out before he can secure multiple kills.

#### **During Siphon Blaster, Damage Output Is Very Low**

Even though he needs it to survive, pulling out Siphon Blaster sacrifices a lot of damage. If his target is receiving healing, he struggles to secure the kill until he swaps back to his rifle or lands a direct hit with ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/cyber-frag.png)Cyber Frag.

#### **Lags Behind in His Niche**

Emre competes with heroes like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/soldier-76.png)Soldier: 76, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sojourn.png)Sojourn, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ashe.png)Ashe for the role of a mid to long-range hitscan on his team. These heroes all have well-rounded kits and high damage output. He can trade blows with Soldier, but compared to Sojourn and Ashe, it is difficult to justify picking Emre over them.

## Emre Ability Tips

### Primary Fire: Synthetic Burst Rifle

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/synthetic-burst-rifle.png)Synthetic Burst Rifle  

* Stay unscoped when you need to make large aim adjustments or are targeting an enemy at close range.
* Designed for frequent scope/unscope play. You can seamlessly swap between the two.
* Unscope when not actively aiming at a specific target so you retain full FoV, and don’t limit your visual information.

### Secondary Fire: Take Aim

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/take-aim-emre.png)Take Aim (Emre)  

* No movement penalty, super fast strafing while ADSing, unlike Ana and Ashe, who slow while aiming.
* Greatly increased effective range and tighter bullet spread. ADS whenever possible.
* Range, accuracy, and movement speed make Emre extremely good at medium to long-range poke fights.

### \[SHIFT\]: Siphon Blaster

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/siphon-blaster.png)Siphon Blaster  

* Survival tool. Primarily use Siphon Blaster for survive enemy attacks. You become move evasive with increased jump and movement speed, and all damage deal with the blaster generates life steal.
* The increased mobility is not burst mobility or a dash. It’s similar to Bap Jump or Juno glide, and not like Echo, Winston, or Anran’s SHIFT.
* Blaster is semi-automatic, so you need to rapid tap fire.
* Cools off faster if you burst fire, pause, burst fire, pause, just before overheating.

### \[E\]: Cyber Frag

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/cyber-frag.png)Cyber Frag  

* The main reason you would play ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/emre.png)Emre over another hero like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sojourn.png)Sojourn or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/soldier-76.png)Soldier: 76 is for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/cyber-frag.png)Cyber Frag.
* * The best use is pressuring enemies positioned near corners and walls. You can force retreat, cut off their retreat, or bounce the nade to hit behind cover after their retreat.
* Also good for initiating your attack. Throw the nade first, then start firing so that the explosion hits as your bullets hit for big burst damage.
* * Decent AoE damage, but best used for bursting down a single target.
* Can nade jump, including additional jump speed when combining nade jump with Siphon Blaster.

### Ultimate: Override Protocol

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/override-protocol.png)Override Protocol  

* Start by firing a heavy round. There is a cooldown before you can fire another, so spam light rounds until you can fire another heavy.
* Heavy rounds have greatly increased direct hit and AoE damage.
* Heavy rounds can be used for a super-fast rocket jump by shooting the ground or a wall and jumping during the self-knockback.
* Extremely vulnerable while flying during ult. Make sure you either have a Support directly healing you or position behind a barrier. Otherwise, you’ll need to use vertical cover and be a bit more passive with its use.

## Perks

### Minor Perks

#### Suppressive Security  

* Makes Emre’s Ult more lethal by making it easier to land follow-up Heavy shots.
* * Slow the enemy with light rounds, then finish them off with a heavy round.
* Enemies will be more hesitant to challenge your ult, thus making ulting a bit safer.

#### Enhanced Agility  

* Decent for escaping and evading while Siphon Blaster cools off.
* Since the blaster is constantly overheating (without Heat Sink), you can take advantage of moments when you need to let it cool off.
* The 20% increase in movement speed, on top of the base movement buff, makes escaping feel more realistic.

### Major Perks

#### Heat Sink  

* Recommended Major Perk
* Fire more shots for more total time. Also results in greater life steal. If you are confident in aiming Siphon Blaster, this is a great Perk. Allows you to play more aggressively.
* Especially good for dueling, and for when Tanks are pressuring you (easier to land more shots on their large bodies).

#### Cyber Adhesion  

* Transforms Cyber Frag into a cyber semtex.
* Sneak up on an enemy, throw the nade to stick, and immediately ADS headshot. You’ll delete DPS and Supports.

## Maps

Emre performs best on maps with objective areas and sightlines that match his optimal range (e.g., Rialto, Nepal, Esperança). Ideally, he will play near Cyber Frag’s max range, where he can abuse the AoE damage paired with his rifle follow-up shots.

Smaller maps (e.g., Antarctic Peninsula, Oasis, New Junk City) feel cramped for Emre. Although he can get good value from grenades, he may struggle to utilize the rest of his kit effectively.

As long as he can maintain high ground, he performs decently on dive maps (e.g., Dorado, Watchpoint: Gibraltar), but he gets countered by dive heroes like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/d-va.png)D.Va that are typically played on these maps.

## Team Comp Synergies

Emre wants a frontline Tank who can set up and hold a position. Tanks like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma are ideal choices, as their barriers protect him, and they have the damage and CC needed to help defend against aggressors.

Since he is fairly flexible as a DPS hero and has consistent output, he can pair with just about any other DPS. The main consideration is whether the other DPS is filling the same role (e.g., ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/soldier-76.png)Soldier: 76, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sojourn.png)Sojourn). If the enemy team is countering with dive or a barrier Tank, your team will lack counterplay.

For Supports, it is generally up to Emre to play within line of sight or healing range. Because he has some self-sustain, he can work with both high-healing and utility-based Supports.

## How to Counter Emre 

To counter ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/emre.png)Emre, your first objective should be neutralizing his grenade. You can do this either by playing outside of its range (snipers like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/widowmaker.png)Widowmaker, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ana.png)Ana, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/zenyatta.png)Zenyatta) or by using damage mitigation to block it (e.g., ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/defense-matrix.png)Defense Matrix, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/barrier-shield.png)Barrier Shield).

Emre’s weakest matchup is against dive and rush comps that can chase him down and beat him at close range. He does not have a strong escape tool, and he has no CC, so he is an easy target to play aggressively into.

If you don’t have good burst damage, try to escape during his Siphon Blaster, so you minimize how much life steal he can generate.

Emre has no crowd control counters other than trying to dodge with Siphon Blasters. He’s an easy kill if you can stun or knock him around.

Listen for his Ult voice line, and prepare to shoot Emre out of the sky.
