# Overwatch Overwatch Kiriko Guide and Overview

In this Overwatch Kiriko guide, you'll learn the fundamentals of their abilities, unique play styles, and fundamental tips. Master ability combos, positioning, and matchup knowledge to excel with other Overwatch [heroes](https://mobalytics.gg/overwatch/heroes/kiriko-guide/overwatch/heroes)!

## Kiriko Overview

The ninjutsu master of the Shimada clan, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/kiriko.png)Kiriko, fights swiftly for peace and against injustice. She’s a well-rounded, versatile Support who provides both defensive and offensive support. 

When teamfights get scrambly, and it's every player for themselves, Kiri survives and thrives in the chaos. Her mobility enables her to quickly reposition to escape danger or help an ally secure elims. Generally, she’ll position herself as the mid-line to ensure she’s always within Swift Step and lock-on healing range.

### TLDR

#### Play As

* Play within ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/swift-step.png)Swift Step and Healing Ofuda lock-on range at all times so you can quickly save allies or escape danger without overcommitting.
* Rotate between healing and Kunai pressure to build ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kitsune-rush.png)Kitsune Rush quickly while still threatening enemy backliners with headshots.
* Save ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/protection-suzu.png)Protection Suzu for high-impact debuffs or lethal moments rather than using it purely for healing value.

#### Play Against

* Force out ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/swift-step.png)Swift Step or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/protection-suzu.png)Protection Suzu early, then re-engage while those cooldowns are unavailable.
* Pressure her from multiple angles to deny safe teleport targets and limit her escape options.
* Treat ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kitsune-rush.png)Kitsune Rush like the best offensive ult the enemy team has. Save defensive utility to survive, retreat when possible, or use your own ultimates to prevent her from overwhelming your team.

## Strengths And Weaknesses Summarized

* Auto-tracking healing
* Good survivability
* Strong Ultimate

* Slow projectiles
* Weak vs tanks
* Difficult to use primary fire

## Strengths And Weaknesses Explained

### Strengths

#### Auto-tracking healing

No matter your skill level, everyone misses shots. Kiri never has to worry about that with her forgiving lock-on hitbox. Once locked on, the ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/healing-ofuda.png)Healing Ofuda will even chase allies around corners. Comes especially in handy with small and mobile teammates.

#### High survivability

Supports are often the first players targeted by the enemy team. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/kiriko.png)Kiriko is one of the most difficult Supports to eliminate because she has so many survival tools. Kunai counts as well since it poses a decent threat to attackers.

#### Fight-winning Support Ultimate

Most Support ults need to be comboed with a Tank or DPS ult to win a fight. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kitsune-rush.png)Kitsune Rush empowers the whole team and is often enough of an advantage all on its own to win the teamfight. 

### Weaknesses

#### Slow healing projectile and limited lock-on range

The farther away an ally gets from ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/kiriko.png)Kiriko, the more noticeable her slow healing becomes. At max lock-on range, the healing can arrive too late to save them. Additionally, compared to a lot of other Supports, Healing Ofuda’s Lock on range is very limited so Kiri falls behind in ranged capabilities.

#### Weak into barrier Tanks

Barriers block both ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kunai.png)Kunai and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/healing-ofuda.png)Healing Ofuda. If a Barrier is between Kiri and her allies, Kunai deals far too little damage to destroy it. She needs to take the extra time to constantly reposition and play around barriers.

#### Difficult to use primary fire

The slow travel speed, rate of fire, and low damage of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kunai.png)Kunai hinder Kiri’s offensive prowess. Landing headshots brings big value but is very inconsistent, even for the best aimers. 

## Kiriko Ability Tips

### Primary Fire: Healing Ofuda

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/healing-ofuda.png)Healing Ofuda  

* Focus on mobile allies
* * When your team has highly mobile heroes like Genji, Tracer or Doomfist, be sure to prioritize healing them since your other Support may struggle to consistently heal them.
* Auto-tracking range
* * ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/swift-step.png)Swift Step and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/healing-ofuda.png)Healing Ofuda lock-on effects have the same 35m range. Therefore, if you see the Swift Step indicator around an ally, you know they are in range for the auto-tracking ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/healing-ofuda.png)Healing Ofuda.
* Always active projectile
* * Even if an ally isn’t being locked on, they will still absorb and be healed by the ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/healing-ofuda.png)Healing Ofudaif they come into contact with it. This applies when teammates walk in front of one another or when they are outside of the lock-on range.
* Doesn’t penetrate barriers
* * Don’t forget that deployable barriers like Winston bubble or Sigma shield will block your allies from receiving your healing.

### Secondary Fire: Kunai

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kunai.png)Kunai  

* Kunai between healing
* * Kiri has time to fire one Kunai between every ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/healing-ofuda.png)Healing Ofuda without sacrificing healing output. This increases her overall damage output and ult charge generation.
* Go for headshots
* * Even though you may miss more Kunai, the burst damage from landing a single critical hit is greater than hitting two body shots in a row.
* Infinite range
* * Kiri is good at pressuring the enemy backline because she has no falloff damage. Try shooting a few shots at the enemy Ana, Ashe, Zen, and other heroes who sit far behind their team.

### \[Shift\]: Swift Step

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/swift-step.png)Swift Step  

* Keep track of ally positions
* * If you don’t know where an in-range ally is positioned, you won’t be able to quickly use ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/swift-step.png)Swift Step when being attacked. Be sure to know where and if any teammates are in range.
* Teleport to safety
* * 99% of the time, you’ll want to TP to where enemies are not able to attack you so that you’re more likely to survive until your next Swift Step

### \[E\]: Protection Suzu

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/protection-suzu.png)Protection Suzu  

* Save when the enemy team has debuffs or negative effects
* * ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ana.png)Ana Anti-Nade, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/junker-queen.png)Junker Queen wounds & anti, and Ashe or Mauga burning, are all examples of abilities worth using Suzu on.  
   * If Junker Queen sees you use ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/protection-suzu.png)Protection Suzu just for healing, she knows she can get an easy team wipe with her ultimate Carnage.
* Swift Step Suzu combo
* * When a teammate is outside of your LoS or behind a wall, AND you have ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/protection-suzu.png)Protection Suzu available, you can TP to them and save them with Suzu and healing.
* Doesn’t cleanse certain abilities like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/charge.png)Charge or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/earthshatter.png)Earthshatter, but the brief invincibility will prevent them if timed perfectly.

### Ultimate: Kitsune Rush

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kitsune-rush.png)Kitsune Rush  

* Memorize Kitsune Counters
* * Aside from healing Support ults like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/transcendence.png)Transcendence, be cautious of AoE ults like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/tectonic-shock.png)Tectonic Shock, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/molten-core.png)Molten Core, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/self-destruct.png)Self-Destruct, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/downpour.png)Downpour, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/dragonstrike.png)Dragonstrike, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/earthshatter.png)Earthshatter. Ults like these will either hit your whole team, or force everyone to leave ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kitsune-rush.png)Kitsune Rush's AoE
* Kitsune alone or combo?
* * If your team has ults like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/tactical-visor.png)Tactical Visor or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/dragonblade.png)Dragonblade, save your ult to combo with your allies. Doing so greatly increases the power of both ults.  
   * If your team doesn’t have a specific combo, then use Kitsune on its own as it can easily be a one-ult fight win if the enemy doesn’t have counter ults available.

### (Passive): Wall Climb

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/wall-climb.png)Wall Climb  

* Get LoS without committing
* * Instead of using Swift Step to assist a teammate behind a wall, Kiri can climb and shoot or throw Suzu over the wall, then immediately fall back behind cover.
* Additional survival tool
* * When combined and rotated with Suzu and Swift Step, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/wall-climb.png)Wall Climb makes ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/kiriko.png)Kiriko extremely evasive. Most enemies aren’t going to be able to chase you if you climb up and over a wall.

## Perks

### Minor Perks

#### Urgent Care  

_“Healing Ofuda projectile speed is increased by 50% when seeking critical health allies.”_

* Take if you’ve already noticed your team demanding a lot of healing.
* * Receiving healing sooner when at critical health can mean the difference between life or death for your ally. The farther they are from you, the greater benefit the Perk gives.

#### Fortune Teller  

_“Kunai hits launch 2 Healing Ofuda to an ally in front of you.”_

* Free healing when attacking
* * If you are able to be aggressive and attack frequently, Fortune Teller is the better Perk to take.  
   * With the Fortune teller perk, it's worth shooting two Kunai between every ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/healing-ofuda.png)Healing Ofuda. For a slight delay in primary fire, she has the chance to deal damage and increase healing potential from the extra 2 Ofudas per hit.

### Major Perks

Ready Step

_“Swift Step grants Kiriko 40% increased attack and reload speed for 3 seconds."_

* When selecting Ready Step, your dives become more lethal. Wait to TP to your ally until they need assistance with an elimination.
* Be smart. Just because you get buffed offense, doesn't mean you should always TP aggressively. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/swift-step.png)Swift Step is you best escape tool, so if you TP into multiple enemies, you likely won't escape.

#### Foxtrot  

_“Protection Suzu grants 30% movement speed to allies for 4 seconds.”_

* 30% movement speed is really strong, especially on heroes who need to stay close to deal optimal damage.
* * For example, when playing with ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/reinhardt.png)Reinhardt the movement speed allows him to chase down squishies that would otherwise be just barely outside his range.
* Better disengage. Faster movement speed enables you and your allies to escape more effectively when using![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/protection-suzu.png)Protection Suzu defensively.

## Maps

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/kiriko.png)Kiriko's mobility and versatility make her a solid choice on every map. But because she can climb and teleport through walls, maps with lots of structures or rooms give her more outplay potential. Long sight lines are good for her Kunai, but bad for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/healing-ofuda.png)Healing Ofuda if her allies get too far away from her. Be mindful of the map area you are in and adjust accordingly. Just make sure to always be within lock-on and Swift Step range to your team.

### Best maps

* Dorado
* King’s Row
* Colosseo

### Worst Maps

* Hanaoka
* Junkertown
* Oasis

## Team Comp Synergies

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/kiriko.png)Kiriko has great self-survivability, range, and movement. These qualities mean she pairs best with rush and dive comps, but her versatile kit will allow her to find value in every team comp.

Tanks that want to position and move aggressively are good choices. Especially those without barriers or an absorption to block status effects like anti-![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/biotic-grenade.png)Biotic Grenade. For example, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/mauga.png)Mauga benefits greatly from ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/kiriko.png)Kiriko because he has no way to block such abilities, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kitsune-rush.png)Kitsune Rush paired with ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/cage-fight.png)Cage Fight is a deadly combo.

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/kiriko.png)Kiriko pairs best with mid and frontline DPS. Thanks to her ranged healing and damage, she wants to always face forward and constantly spam, and never have to turn around. Snipers are her worst pairings since she needs to turn away from the main action to peel for snipers.

She can be played as the primary source of sustain, or focus more on utility. Historically, her best pairing has been ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/lucio.png)Lúcio because they both are able to play hyper-aggressively, survive without relying on the other, and synergize with the same team comps. 

## How to Counter Kiriko 

Force out ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/swift-step.png)Swift Step or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/protection-suzu.png)Protection Suzu early, then re-engage while those cooldowns are unavailable.

Pressure her from multiple angles to deny safe teleport targets and limit her escape options.

Treat ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kitsune-rush.png)Kitsune Rush like the best offensive ult the enemy team has. Save defensive utility to survive, retreat when possible, or use your own ultimates to prevent her from overwhelming your team.
