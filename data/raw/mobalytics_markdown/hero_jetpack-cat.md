# Overwatch Overwatch Jetpack Cat Guide and Overview

In this Overwatch Jetpack Cat guide, you'll learn the fundamentals of their abilities, unique play styles, and fundamental tips. Master ability combos, positioning, and matchup knowledge to excel with other Overwatch [heroes](https://mobalytics.gg/overwatch/heroes/jetpack-cat-guide/overwatch/heroes)!

## Jetpack Cat Overview

A stray cat found in Gibraltar and adopted by Brigitte. She pilots a jetpack and is ready to sacrifice all nine lives to protect their team. 

Jetpack Cat flies themselves and allies around the map with their unmatched mobility. Their kit benefits from team coordination, sneaky taxiing, and creative playmaking. Lifeline transportation enables unique positioning strategies that have never before been possible in the history of Overwatch! 

With high mobility, AoE aura healing, and limited primary fire damage/healing range, Jetpack Cat wants to hover just above the battle. Stay close, but not too close, and reposition frequently to keep your enemies guessing as you maximize your jetpack’s value.

### TLDR

#### Play As

* Fly outside of their range when faced against melee and close combat enemies such as Vendetta.
* Keep repositioning around vertical cover, walls, and structures to keep yourself out of enemy hitscan’s LoS.
* Target Tanks when going to environmental KOs with your ult. When there are no ledges, wait for an ally to use a high damage Ult like Death Blossom.

#### Play Against

* Hitscan heroes are strong against Jetpack Cat. If they are giving you trouble, consider swapping.
* Jetpack Cat doesn’t generate a lot of sustain for their team. If your team has higher healing (Supports like Kiriko and Moira), you win team fights by slowly chipping away at their health while keeping your team alive.
* Immediately start shooting Jetpack Cat when they capture you or a teammate to stop them from getting an environmental KO.

## Strengths And Weaknesses Explained

### Strengths

#### Very Mobile

Extremely mobile. Only hero with permanent flying capabilities, and their boost is very fast, and refreshes quickly. Allows her follow up with teammates everywhere or escape defensively. 

#### Transportation 

Unique playmaking capabilities by transporting allies around the map. Flying ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/deadeye.png)Deadeye, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/b-o-b.png)B.O.B., and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/tactical-visor.png)Tactical Visor are just a few of the popular ways to combo ally abilities with ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/lifeline.png)Lifeline.

#### Versatility 

Versatile and capable of both defensive and offensive playstyles. Her healing output is good when playing like a Support, but she's also one of the best solo-kill assassins in the game. Even after the nerfs, a Claws Out \+ Territorial will eliminate ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/tracer.png)Tracer without even firing a single shot.

### Weaknesses

#### Weak vs range

Weak against hitscans and long-range heroes. Anyone who can shoot Cat out of the air makes it difficult for her to utilize her best ability. 

#### Ult is risky to use

Ult puts you at risk of being KO’d by enemies that were not knocked down. Even the enemy you grab can shoot and kill you after the stun ends.

#### Lacks a defensive ult

Whereas most Support heroes have an ultimate they can use to save their team from death, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/catnapper.png)Catnapper can only be used offensively. Theoretically, she could use it to stun a solo aggressing enemy like an ulting ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/genji.png)Genji, but generally, she doesn't have a way to save her team from enemy ults.

## Jetpack Cat Ability Tips

### Primary Fire: Biotic Pawjecticles

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/biotic-pawjectiles.png)Biotic Pawjectiles  

* No falloff damage, but the projectile only travels 40m before disappearing.
* Pawjectiles are shotgun spread pellets. Each shot is the shape of a cat’s paw. The claws can crit, but the center paw pad cannot crit.
* * Aim towards enemies' necks and upper chest to maximize pellets hit and critical headshot damage.
* Main source of healing and damage. Prioritize healing, and only focus on damage when your teammates are near full HP.

### Secondary Fire: Frenetic Flight

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/frenetic-flight.png)Frenetic Flight  

* Use the boost to control space and positioning. Optimal range is keeping enemies within primary fire range (40m), and allies within healing aura when using Purr
* Resource-based. After a momentary cooldown, you can reactivate Flight as long as you have remaining fuel.
* Jetpack Cat is extremely evasive and has some of the best mobility in Overwatch.
* * Boost around frequently. Your base flight speed is slow and makes Jetpack Cat an easy target when floating around in the sky.
* Can shoot during boost.

### \[SHIFT\]: Lifeline

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/lifeline.png)Lifeline  

* Transport and heals allies
* Sling shot Cassidy during Deadeye for a lethal surprise Ult!
* The teammate has to press “Interact” to confirm. (Unlike Lifeweaver’s Life Grip)
* * Give your allies time to confirm. It is difficult to quickly yank them out of a fight if they are already at low HP, but it can be a great rescue tool.
* Can NOT shoot during Lifeline.
* No time limit. You can transport your teammate anywhere on the map if you wish.
* * Your teammate CAN shoot and use their abilities while being transported.

### \[E\]: Purr

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/purr.png)Purr  

* Knockback upon activation
* Healing increases towards the end of the duration, making it more important to be near allies in the 2nd half of its duration.
* Can be used during Flight and Lifeline, to keep yourself alive while evading attacks, repositioning, and escaping.

### Ultimate: Catnapper

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/catnapper.png)Catnapper  

* On maps with ledges nearby the objective, save your Ult to use when an environmental KO is possible.
* Low damage, so not lethal on its own. Try to combo with another teammate’s burst damage Ultimate, like Anran’s Ascent.
* Keep in mind that even if you slam into knockdown multiple enemies, if the opposing team has hitscans like Ana and Emre elsewhere on the map, they can easily KO you as you pick up and carry their teammate.
* * Use Purr and Flight immediately after ulting to stay alive.
* Bonus Tip: Against Tanks like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/d-va.png)D.Va who fly after being dropped, don't detach. Instead, use Descend to dip them below the environmental KO threshold off the ledge, then fly away immediately after.

### Passive: Jetpack

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/jetpack.png)Jetpack  

* Just because you can perma-fly at the top of the ceiling, doesn’t mean you should.
* Make sure you stay in range of your own abilities.
* Flying too high where there is no environmental cover to hide behind makes you an easy target and will get you killed quickly.

## Perks

### Minor Perks

#### Ulterior Motive  

* Refills fuel even during ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/frenetic-flight.png)Frenetic Flight.
* Significantly increases mobility and flight uptime when you are healing.
* Dealing Damage does not convert into fuel.
* Recommended default Minor Perk

#### Transport Shielding  

* Best for strategic play-making. Allows you to take more damage while transporting an ally for a flank play. Also makes it safer to go for environmental KOs after your Ult.
* Recommended when playing with comms or frequently coordinating transport plays.

### Major Perks

#### Headbutt  

* Need to have almost half fuel and to travel \~8m to build enough momentum for the boop. (Displayed as VFX circling your screen)
* Works in transport mode
* Pair with Ulterior Motive for more frequent use.

#### Claws Out  

* Best for consistent aggressive plays. If your team is using an aggressive closerange strategy such as brawl, rush or dive, having extra damage every 6-seconds on your melee is quite powerful.
* When your team is playing more distance or poke styles, you won’t have many opportunities to use Claws Out.
* Recommended Major Perk

## Maps

The best maps for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/jetpack-cat.png)Jetpack Cat have plenty of vertical structures for her to fly around. High ground is especially strong since she can perma-fly. Numbani, Watchpoint: Gibraltar, and Paraiso, for example, all feature structural layouts that enable her to constantly reposition safely. 

Flatter maps and those with wide open objective areas such as Samoa, Havana, and New Queen Street don't offer nearly as much room to play. On these maps, you'll typically need to position closer to your team and use them for safety.

## Team Comp Synergies

Jetpack Cat is extremely self-sufficient thanks to their combat ability, mobility, and self-sustain. Because of this, she doesn't need allies to sit around and protect her.

Heroes who can fly or move quickly are no problem to keep up with, so look for teammates like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/d-va.png)D.Va, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/echo.png)Echo, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/tracer.png)Tracer and be ready to ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/frenetic-flight.png)Frenetic Flight in to assist in their attacks.

For a second Support it depends on the rest of your team more than the specific pairing. If your team has a tank like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/mauga.png)Mauga or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/reinhardt.png)Reinhardt who demand a lot of healing resources, it's best to pair with someone like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ana.png)Ana or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/baptiste.png)Baptiste.

However, if your team is running full dive or heroes with self-sustain, you can get away with pairing with a lower healing output Support like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/lucio.png)Lúcio or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/brigitte.png)Brigitte.

## How to Counter Jetpack Cat

To coutner ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/jetpack-cat.png)Jetpack Cat, you need to pressure her every time she exposes herself. Even a bit a damage will force her to back off, which is especially good when she's using ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/purr.png)Purr.

Always be ready for a flank attack because she can fly in super fast and shoot you from the side. Don't tunnel vision, and if you have any CC, you can save it if you've noticed the Cat player keeps going for flanks.

Also, you can counterswap in your role to heroes like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/d-va.png)D.Va, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ashe.png)Ashe or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/illari.png)Illari to have an easier time shooter ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/jetpack-cat.png)Jetpack Cat out of the sky, evading her ult, and shutting down her flanks.
