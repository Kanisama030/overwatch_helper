# Overwatch Overwatch Orisa Guide and Overview

In this Overwatch Orisa guide, you'll learn the fundamentals of their abilities, unique play styles, and fundamental tips. Master ability combos, positioning, and matchup knowledge to dominate with Anran!

## Orisa Overview

Created to be the defender of Numbani, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa holds the front line better than anyone else. She is arguably the most difficult Tank to kill and has decent damage to go along with that survivability. While she lags way behind in mobility, her long-range damage outclasses the rest of the Tank roster. Good Orisa players learn how to cycle her cooldowns and utilize cover when waiting for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify to come back online. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa is a good choice if you want to play on the objective and constantly attack.

### TLDR

#### Play As

* Rotate and maintain distance. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa should always move around the objective with her team, trying to keep the enemy team away.
* Don’t use all her abilities together. Use just ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/javelin-spin.png)Javelin Spin, then after it ends, use ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify, then ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/energy-javelin.png)Energy Javelin. Using them together greatly reduces her survivability.
* Always play near cover. Because there will always be gaps between ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify uses, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa needs cover to hide behind to stay alive.

#### Play Against

* Ignore ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa. The best way to counter ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa is to avoid her and target the rest of her team. She’ll try to stop you, but she can’t chase you down.
* Wait out ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify. If you see her golden, either hide so you don’t take damage, or switch targets, and attack someone else.
* Go beams. If all else fails, the easiest way to counter ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa is to play a beam hero. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa is free ult charge to them.

## Strengths And Weaknesses Explained

### Strengths

#### Fortify

Becoming unstoppable and reducing all incoming damage by nearly half is ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa's greatest strength. She's the only tank who can simultaneously be CC immune while walking forward and attacking.

#### Great counterpick

Lore accurate, she counters ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist through her crowd control, and with ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify's knockback immunity. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa is also an excellent counterswap to shut down ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/reinhardt.png)Reinhardt, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ramattra.png)Ramattra, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/roadhog.png)Roadhog, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/hazard.png)Hazard.

#### Ranged damage

Most tanks have either very low or zero long-range damage. In the poke phases of fights, or from medium to long distance, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa will be bringing more value to her team than any other Tank.

### Weaknesses

#### Vulnerable to beams

Because ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/javelin-spin.png)Javelin Spin does not block beam damage like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/zarya.png)Zarya and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/symmetra.png)Symmetra, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa becomes weak against beams anytime she doesn't have ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify available. 

#### No team damage mitigation

Unlike most other Tanks, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa doesn't provide any overhealth or barriers for her team. She can only absorb attacks with her own body or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/javelin-spin.png)Javelin Spin, but doesn't directly bring any extra survivability to her teammates.

#### Immobile

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa's biggest weakness is being slowed while firing, having no vertical mobility, and just a tiny bit of movement buff from ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/javelin-spin.png)Javelin Spin. Heroes with a lot of mobility can simply avoid ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa, and she can't do much about it.

## Orisa Ability Tips

### Primary Fire: Augmented Fusion Driver

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/augmented-fusion-driver.png)Augmented Fusion Driver  

* Keep your distance and poke
* * ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa has better ranged damage than any other Tank. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/mauga.png)Mauga can compete, but with ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify, his burning gun doesn’t even crit ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa anyway. Deal as much damage as possible before letting the enemy Tank close the distance on you.
* Don’t overheat
* * It’s always better to stop shooting just before overheating than to get hit with the 3-second overheated penalty.

### Secondary Fire: Energy Javelin

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/energy-javelin.png)Energy Javelin  

* Play keep away
* * Since ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa generally outranges the rest of the Tanks, she wants to use ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/javelin-spin.png)Javelin Spin to keep the enemy pushed back and away from her.
* Peel for your backline
* * The knockback of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/energy-javelin.png)Energy Javelin is a strong tool for keeping any enemy away from your backline. If you see an enemy Tank or DPS attacking your team behind you, try to hit them with the ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/energy-javelin.png)Energy Javelin.
* Wall collisions
* * ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/energy-javelin.png)Energy Javelin deals extra stun and damage when it knocks back the enemy into a wall. Try to take advantage of this by rotating around your target so they are between you and a wall or structure.

### \[SHIFT\]: Fortify

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify  

* ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa's most important ability
* * ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify makes ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa nearly unkillable and literally unstoppable. When using ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa can press forward, counter CC, and force the enemy team to fall back.  
   * ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify has a long cooldown, so it’s important not to waste. Only activate ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify when the enemy team has to focus you because you are being an active threat through your damage and positioning.
* Use while you still have Armor
* * Using ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify while you still have armor stacks the damage reduction (up to 50%). Additionally, you’ll be more likely to still have Armor health when ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify ends if you still had some when you first activated it because of this.  
   * If ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify ends and you have no Armor, RUN!
* Counter Ults
* * Save ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify when the enemy has stun Ultimates like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/blizzard.png)Blizzard, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/graviton-surge.png)Graviton Surge, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/gravitic-flux.png)Gravitic Flux, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/downpour.png)Downpour, and similar.

### \[E\]: Javelin Spin

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/javelin-spin.png)Javelin Spin  

* Cycle with Fortify
* * Don’t use ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/javelin-spin.png)Javelin Spin while using ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify. You need to use these two survival tools separately, or the enemy has a massive window of time to kill ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa.
* Secure elims
* * Since ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/javelin-spin.png)Javelin Spin blocks enemy healing and damage projectiles while also CC’ing the enemy, it’s quite effective at finishing off low HP targets.  
   * Get between your target and their Supports, then activate ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/javelin-spin.png)Javelin Spin to push them towards your team
* What doesn’t Spin block?
* * Doesn’t block beams or splash damage, and doesn’t destroy airborne deployables like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/symmetra.png)Symmetra's turrets before they land.

### Ultimate: Terra Surge

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/terra-surge.png)Terra Surge  

* How long should I charge ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/terra-surge.png)Terra Surge?
* * You need to channel ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa Ult for about 3 seconds to one-shot DPS and Supports.
* Ignores barriers
* * Slam damage cannot be blocked by barriers like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/reinhardt.png)Reinhardt Shield.
* Breaking LoS stops damage
* * LoS can be used to prevent damage. So any environmental structures or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/mei.png)Mei and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/hazard.png)Hazard's walls can block the damage for example.

   * Always jump before activating, but whenever possible, use the payload or environment to get extra height.

Moreover, the speed boost during and after, plus the CC is stronger than having a shield.

#### Protective Barrier  

* Match up dependent
* * Against ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/mauga.png)Mauga, it could be considered the better Major Perk since it can be used to help counter ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/cardiac-overdrive.png)Cardiac Overdrive.

   * In theory, you can use the Barrier to block ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ramattra.png)Ramattra's ult LoS and try to end it early. The 600HP barrier may not survive long enough to rely on this tech, however.
* Double Barrier tip
* * When on defense or when the enemy team is returning from spawn, shoot the Barrier straight up. By the time it lands, you’ll only have 3 seconds before your next Barrier.

## Maps

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa is always going to be a good choice on maps that favor poke comps. An objective area with wide-open space and long, straight payload routes that play into her strengths. Because she’s very tanky, she’s not bad on small maps either, but it makes it harder for her to execute her keepaway game plan. She can contest high ground with her ranged damage, but she can’t ever access the high ground herself, so she’s also at a disadvantage on maps with lots of high ground that favor dive comps.

### Best Maps

* Junkertown
* Midtown
* New Queen Street

### Worst Maps

* Dorado
* Numbani
* Hanaoka

## Team Comp Synergies

Pair ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa with other poke heroes and shut down the enemy team before they can even get close to your team. She's also an excellent protector, so when the enemy team is running dive or rush, she can be a nuisance to them, stop all CC, knock them around, and keep her team safe to attack freely. 

Since she's pretty stationary, it's easy for Supports like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/baptiste.png)Baptiste and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/illari.png)Illari to keep her health nice and full.

## How to Counter Orisa 

To counter Orisa, you need to exploit gaps in her cooldown cycles. She's virtually unkillable if she has ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/javelin-spin.png)Javelin Spin available. If you see her use both of these cooldowns, you have a short window where she's vulnerable. Go aggressive quickly before the Fortify cooldown refreshes.

In order to pull this off, you need to have the resources still available after she's used hers. Therefore, as soon as you see her activate ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify, focus on defending, backing up, and saving cooldowns. Then, you will have everything you need to punish her and win the fight.
