# Overwatch Overwatch Domina Guide and Overview

In this Overwatch Domina guide, you'll learn the fundamentals of their abilities, unique play styles, and fundamental tips. Master ability combos, positioning, and matchup knowledge to excel with other Overwatch [heroes](https://mobalytics.gg/overwatch/heroes/domina-guide/overwatch/heroes)!

## Domina Overview

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina is a barrier poke tank with great ranged damage. Rotating around the objective and using the environment to control distance from the enemy frontline are critical to finding success with her kit. 

In ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/tank.svg)Tank matchups, she excels at medium-range poke battles. Most of the other ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/tank.svg)Tanks beat her at close-range, but she has crowd control abilities to help her dictate the pace of the battle.

Teammates with similar ranged capabilities are great pairings, because they’ll get a lot of benefit from her massive barrier. Overall, as long as she can control spacing, she’s at an advantage, but heavy dive and brawl comps are difficult for her to deal with if she’s unable to outrun them.

### TLDR

#### Play As

* Always play near cover. Peek out for the beam burst, and quickly hide between shots.
* Use your range to target DPS and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/support.svg)Supports to get a quick elim or force them to retreat.
* Position around the perimeter of objectives so you can contest without getting surrounded.

#### Play Against

* AoE and explosive damage can be used to destroy multiple barrier panels at once.
* Dive counters her playstyle. Overwhelm her by quickly getting behind her barrier.
* If a teammate gets imprisoned by her ult. Help them destroy the barrier from the outside.

## Strengths And Weaknesses Summarized

* Sightline Control
* High damage
* Fight-winning Ult

* Countered by shield busters
* Vulnerable to Brawl
* Weak vs dive

## Strengths And Weaknesses Explained

### Strengths

#### Space and sightline control

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/barrier-array.png)Barrier Array arc shape and segmented panels of health make it one of the best abilities in Overwatch for controlling sightlines. Allies are able to take wider angles behind the barrier and reposition as panels are broken. The enemy team generally won't be able to take out all panels. At chokes and in long narrow hallways map areas, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina's barrier gives her team a huge advantage in the poke battle.

#### High damage from medium range

The burst damage and range of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/photon-magnum.png)Photon Magnum make her an extremely lethal threat at mid-range. The beam is the longest in the game, enabling her to play outside the optimal range of close-range enemies. Enemies positioned behind their Tank are often caught off guard by her deceptive range when she lands a crit with the explosion.

#### Fight-winning Ultimate

Many heroes lack the damage required to break through ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/panopticon.png)Panopticon before the barrier explodes. Unless their teammates break them out, catching a couple Supports with her Ult can secure the fight win all on its own.

### Weaknesses

#### Countered by shield buster

Team comps with heroes like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/bastion.png)Bastion or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/junkrat.png)Junkrat who excel at burning through barrier health leath ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina with very little counterplay. They can play outside her range, and take away her only form of safety. 

#### Vulnerable to brawl Tanks

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina beats brawl Tanks by keeping her distance and controlling the spacing. When she's unable to do so, her only close-range tool is![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/sonic-repulsors.png)Sonic Repulsors knockback. One brawl Tanks like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/reinhardt.png)Reinhardt and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/zarya.png)Zarya get up close and personal, she quickly falls.

#### Weak against dive 

With no mobility tools, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina is unable to keep pace with high mobility dive heroes like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/winston.png)Winston, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/tracer.png)Tracer. They can easily keep away from her, and she struggles to peel for her backline when they bypass her. Also, she offers little help to her own high mobility allies.

## Domina Ability Tips

### Primary Fire: Photon Magnum

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/photon-magnum.png)Photon Magnum  

* Longest beam in OW. Although it has a limited range of 25m, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/photon-magnum.png)Photon Magnum has a greater range than any other beam in the game.
* ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina has the range advantage in all ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/tank.svg)Tank matchups other than ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/orisa.png)Orisa, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/mauga.png)Mauga, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ramattra.png)Ramattra in Omnic Form
* Burst shot at the end is hitscan. No need to lead the shot.
* The burst and range make ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina especially effective at jiggle peeking while her barrier is on cooldown.
* * Briefly position behind cover between shots and during beam startup. Only peek out to shoot just before the high-impact shot fires. Doing so will minimize the time she can take damage, while still maintaining high dps output.

### Secondary Fire: Barrier Array

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/barrier-array.png)Barrier Array  

* Deploy the semi-circle barrier in front of choke points and in hallways with side doors. This maximizes the sightlines your team is protected from and can attack through.
* After choosing a placement for the barrier, each panel has its own HP, and must be destroyed individually or will expire at max duration.
* ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/barrier-array.png)Barrier Array is weak against AoE damage that can destroy multiple panels at once. Examples include ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/junkrat.png)Junkrat mines and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/reinhardt.png)Reinhardt ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-hammer.png)Rocket Hammer.

### \[SHIFT\]: Sonic Repulsors

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/sonic-repulsors.png)Sonic Repulsors  

* Wall slams stun and inflict bonus damage. Be aware of the surrounding environment and try to position the enemy between ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina and a wall or structure, such as a payload.
* Against melee heroes, use this as a “get off me” button. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina doesn’t want to be point-blank. She wants to use her Crystal and beam range.
* When possible, look for environmental KO opportunities, such as on Eichenwald’s bridge.

### \[E\]: Crystal Charge

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/crystal-charge.png)Crystal Charge  

* Extremely good for restoring shields with her passive ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/reconstruction.png)Reconstruction.
* * When your HP is low, target deployables like an enemy ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina's barrier to rapidly deal ability damage and burst heal yourself.
* Nearly infinite range (100 meters). Use it as soon as you see enemies while they are outside of your beam’s range.
* Upon impact with hero or terrain, the Crystal deals damage over time.
* Can be manually detonated in the air. Time detonation to explode right on top of enemies.
* Pierces through barriers so you can shoot ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/tank.svg)Tanks like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma while they are protected from your beam.

### Ultimate: Panopticon

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/panopticon.png)Panopticon  

* How the explosive damage works
* * Barrier HP: 450  
   * Explosion damage scales from 100 – 300 based on barrier HP on expiration  
   * The hero inside can damage it, and their teammates outside the barrier can destroy it as well.
* Best used on ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/support.svg)Supports and other heroes with low barrier damage capabilities.
* * ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/kiriko.png)Kiriko and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ana.png)Ana, for example, both deal very low damage to barriers and will likely be instakilled by the explosion unless outside teammates help destroy its health.  
   * On the other hand, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/roadhog.png)Roadhog will quickly burn through it, and can use ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/take-a-breather.png)Take a Breather to further reduce damage taken.
* Keep attacking!
* * Imprisoned enemies can’t attack you, but they still take damage from ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina and her teammates. If you get the low, the explosion will often be enough to secure the elim.

### Passive: Reconstruction

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/reconstruction.png)Reconstruction  

* Only ability damage direct to enemy heroes counts towards ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/reconstruction.png)Reconstruction shield restoration.
* When your shields are low, keep an eye on how much health you have as you deal ability damage. When you are able to deal more damage, you’ll be able to stay in the fight longer.

## Perks

### Minor Perks

#### Efficient Design  

* Best for defensive play.
* When using Efficient Design, always wait to take a bit of damage before deploying her barrier.

Extended Power  

* Better for offense.
* Extending your primary fire by 5m is very powerful; do not underestimate the strength of this minor perk.
* After activating, experiment with the increased range. Keep rotating during fights, and abuse your superior range when battling ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/tank.svg)Tanks.
* Ignore the enemy ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/tank.svg)Tank and use your Beam to Target squishy targets who think they are safe.

### Major Perks

#### Disruptive Detonation  

* Aim to detonate ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/crystal-charge.png)Crystal Charge between two enemies to maximize the perk’s CC effect.
* Slow down the enemy ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/tank.svg)Tank's approach, and hinder enemies trying to escape.
* Recommended Major Perk

#### Power Move  

* Good to give ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina counterplay against melee heroes.
* Once the enemy is in melee range, first deploy barrier behind them, then use ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/sonic-repulsors.png)Sonic Repulsors to wall slam them into your barrier.
* Power Move only causes barrier to act as a wall for Sonic wall slams. Enemies can still freely walk and move through it as usual.

## Maps

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina performs best on maps with strong choke points and long hallways, such as **Havana**, **Junkertown**, and **Midtown**. These maps favor poke compositions, which Domina pairs well with and counters effectively thanks to her high barrier uptime and ranged attacks.

She struggles most with two types of maps. The first are those with very tight spaces, such as **New Junk City** and **Antarctic Peninsula**. While she can work with the right comps—provided you rotate and maintain optimal distance—it can be a significant struggle.

Maps with an abundance of high ground, such as **Watchpoint: Gibraltar** and **Numbani**, give her the most trouble. As stated under "Weaknesses" above, Domina struggles against high-mobility and dive heroes who thrive on these vertical layouts.

## Team Comp Synergies

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina pairs well with heroes who need a large shield to stand behind while spamming ranged damage. Heroes like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sojourn.png)Sojourn, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ashe.png)Ashe, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/baptiste.png)Baptiste share similar positioning strategies. Their poke pressure helps her control spacing and chip away at the enemy team's resources before they can fully approach. Since she lacks mobility, AoE and projectile healing provide the most consistent sustain.

## How to Counter Domina 

The best way to counter ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina is to use heroes who can trade cooldowns to burn through her barrier. Aside from minor shield generation from ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/reconstruction.png)Reconstruction, Domina doesn't have survivability tools outside of her barrier. Heroes like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/bastion.png)Bastion can use ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/configuration-assault.png)Configuration: Assaultt to quickly shred the shield, exposing Domina and her allies to their team's onslaught of damage.

Alternatively, you can bypass her barrier altogether using Rush or Dive comps. These strategies use bursts of mobility to close the gap and overwhelm the backline. Success depends on minimizing damage during the approach; for example, if a ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/reinhardt.png)Reinhardt reaches melee range with only 400 barrier health and no armor remaining, he won't be able to swing aggressively enough to win the fight.

## Role Passives: Tank and Stalwart
