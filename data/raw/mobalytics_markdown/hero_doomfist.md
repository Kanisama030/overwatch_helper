# Overwatch Overwatch Doomfist Guide and Overview

In this Overwatch Doomfist guide, you'll learn the fundamentals of their abilities, unique play styles, and fundamental tips. Master ability combos, positioning, and matchup knowledge to excel with other Overwatch [heroes](https://mobalytics.gg/overwatch/heroes/doomfist-guide/overwatch/heroes)!

## Doomfist Overview

The leader of Talon and the main villain of Overwatch is also the main villain of every backline. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist is one of the best disruptors and harassers in the entire game. Equipped with multiple movement abilities, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist is hard to lock down and can jump on you at a moment's notice. 

His knockback and AoE damage make it hard to advance on him, but his mobility allows him to easily approach you. Oftentimes, the only way players can shut down a skilled ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist player is by running multiple direct counters and heroes with lots of crowd control. 

He’s not the best at contesting the objective or frontlining choke points, but his ability to assassinate the enemy's backline more than makes up for those weaknesses. 

### TLDR

#### Play As

* Use your superior mobility to quickly get in and get out. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist should constantly disrupt the enemy and get in the way of their attacks.
* Always save one ability (Punch, Slam, or Block) to survive or disengage from your dive. Dumping all three into a team with full health just results in ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist feeding.
* Track enemy stuns like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/sleep-dart.png)Sleep Dart, and either Slam Cancel Power Block to dodge or don’t Block unless you’ve already seen the stun used.

#### Play Against

* While he’s blocking or charging ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch, stun him. He’s sitting still, and cancelling his ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/power-block.png)Power Block is the best method for eliminating ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist.
* Dodge his engage attack. Keep track of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist and watch for when he’s coming in with ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slam or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch. If you dodge his initial attack, he misses out on overhealth and will have to reset.
* To counter ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist, play heroes with stuns or high mobility heroes who can evade him. If you can’t run away or stun him, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist will harass you all game.

## Strengths And Weaknesses Explained

### Strengths

#### Hyper-mobile

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist has frequent mobility tools that he can use for both horizontal and vertical movement. Nowhere is safe from ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist. He can rapidly traverse the map, bypass the enemy frontline, or retreat to safety. 

#### Crowd Control

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch is on a short 4-second cooldown and can be instantly refreshed by ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/power-block.png)Power Block. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist has more frequent knockback than any other hero. He’s great at controlling space by repeatedly displacing the enemy.

#### Disruption

Even when ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist doesn’t get the kill, his presence is extremely disruptive. When he’s diving a hero or group of enemies, they have to use their cooldowns to counterattack. He takes up a lot of attention, and during that distracted time, the rest of his team has greater opportunities to make big plays of their own. 

### Weaknesses

#### Vulnerable to stuns

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/power-block.png)Power Block has damage reduction, but Doom has no way of actually blocking stuns. During his channelled abilities, he’s barely moving and is an easy target. Since his best moves are movement-based, landing a stun on ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist shuts him down hard.

#### Melee hero

The main limiting factor of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist's kit is that he is primarily a melee hero. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hand-cannon.png)Hand Cannon gives him a bit of range and poke, but it’s more of a supplemental ability to increase the lethality of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slam. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist has to get in super close, and that exposes him to a lot of danger.

#### High skill ceiling

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist is one of those heroes that only shines in the hands of a committed one-trick. If you are just casually playing ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist and he’s not your main, be prepared to have a lot of rough games. He has a multitude of hero-specific tech, and players need to have high game sense to read and track enemy resources. 

## Doomfist Ability Tips

### Primary Fire: Hand Cannon

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hand-cannon.png)Hand Cannon  

* Range
* * ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hand-cannon.png)Hand Cannon’s effective range is about 10 meters. Farther than that, the pellets are too spread out and the damage falloff begins to kick in as well.
* Poke and prefire
* * ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hand-cannon.png)Hand Cannon can be used to get in a bit of poke damage before committing to a dive engagement.  
   * Always fire off one or two ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hand-cannon.png)Hand Cannon before ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punching or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slamming to chip down the enemy's health and deal more overall damage.
* Auto-reloads
* * Keep an eye on your ammo because your primary fire auto-reloads anytime you aren’t firing it.  
   * There is a brief cooldown after using ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hand-cannon.png)Hand Cannon before it begins reloading, so if you need more ammo, don’t fire any shots.

### Secondary Fire: Rocket Punch

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch  

* Engage with Punch
* * The short cooldown and refresh on Empowered ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch make ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch better for engaging than ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slam when you don’t need ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slam's verticality.  
   * Hitting a stun on your first attack also makes your follow-up easier.
* Wall slams
* * You should always be trying to line up the enemies with walls or structures behind them. You can strafe or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slam into position, then ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch into a wall.
* Hold onto empowered
* * When you have Empowered ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch, cancel with primary fire or by using another ability if you don’t have a good target. It’s important not to waste Empowered ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch.

### \[SHIFT\]: Seismic Slam

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slam  

* Get into position
* * Slam is great for jumping over walls or the enemy team to get into position for a ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch.  
   * Just make sure you end up behind cover or somewhere the enemy team can’t kill you if you use both in abilities in a row like this.
* Get out
* * ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slam's fast speed and capability for vertical, diagonal and horizontal movement make it really good for getting out of dangerous positions.  
   * After Punching or Blocking, you’ll often need to Slam away so you can stay alive then hard engage after the enemies' resources are low.
* Slam cancel
* * Activating Block or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch before during your ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slam will cancel the ability but keep the momentum. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slam vertically, then cancel and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist will go flying into the air.

### \[E\]: Power Block

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/power-block.png)Power Block  

* How to increase Empowered rates
* * Wait until the enemy is already attacking. Don’t preemptively ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/power-block.png)Power Block, hoping the enemy will start attacking you.  
   * Block slow travel speed projectiles like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fire-strike.png)Fire Strike and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/pharah.png)Pharah's ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-launcher.png)Rocket Launcher. You have enough time to block these types of projectiles after seeing they’ve been fired at you.  
   * Create pressure. If you’re being a huge threat to the enemy team, they’ll be focus firing at you which results in more damage to Block.
* Bait stuns
* * Cancel with ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slam when you know the enemy is going to try and stun you.  
   * Example: ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ana.png)Ana's ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/sleep-dart.png)Sleep Dart cooldown is twice as long as ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/power-block.png)Power Block and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slam. Therefore, you can use Block, Slam to the side before Sleep hits you, then have both abilities back before ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ana.png)Ana has another ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/sleep-dart.png)Sleep Dart.
* Tank high damage ults
* * Staying alive is actually the main use of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/power-block.png)Power Block. If you don’t have your movement to dodge, use Block to tank high burst damage attacks like Pulse Bomb.

### (Passive): The Best Defense…

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/the-best-defense.png)The Best Defense...  

* Overhealth
* * ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist can generate up to 200 overhealth. This is easiest with ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/meteor-strike.png)Meteor Strike because it generates more shields per enemy hit.  
   * Generating temporary shields is the key to ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist's survival. Carefully aim your abilities to ensure you are constantly gaining the overhealth. If you don’t, you’ll need to disengage much sooner.
* Decay
* * Overhealth doesn’t decay until after 3 seconds, and the decay is slow.  
   * Don’t rush just because you have overhealth. You still have time to carefully plan out your attack path and target.
* Only generated when dealing ability damage
* * Overhealth doesn’t generate from ability use, but only ability damage.  
   * Example: ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch being collision canceled by another knockdown ability (Charge, Bash, Punch, B.O.B.) generates no overhealth.

### Ultimate: Meteor Strike

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/meteor-strike.png)Meteor Strike  

* Refresh cooldowns and health
* * Unless there is a tight group of low HP enemies, it's always better to hold off on activating the Strike until your health and cooldowns have recovered.
* Read movement
* * Wait and watch your target to see how they are moving. They know you are in ult and will try to dodge. Predict which direction they are moving and select that location.
* Get ready to dodge
* * Against enemies with projectile stuns, they often try to hit ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist as soon as he lands. In these matchups consider Slamming away as soon as you land.
* Zoom in vs zoom out
* * When zoomed in, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist stays on his current ground level or drops to the lowest available ground.  
   * When zoomed out, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist will instead move onto the highest targeted floor.

   * Dump all ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hand-cannon.png)Hand Cannon ammo into the enemy, quick ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch into a wall, finish them off with the refreshed primary fire, Slam away.

* * Because you don’t have to spend time charging Empowered ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch, One-Two gives you rapid burst damage potential.

#### Survival of the Fittest  

* A bit weaker overall than One-Two
* ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist gets plenty of elims
* * Because he has AoE abilities, and your team follows up on your engages, most kills by your team will have some damage assist from ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist and count as an elim for the extra overhealth.
* Not good if your team is getting rolled
* * On the occasion you need more solo play-making potential, One-Two would be better to take.  
   * When your team isn’t getting a lot of elims, you won’t get much benefit from choosing Survival of the Fittest

The only thing he requires is natural cover to rotate around. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist typically takes space in chunks so long sight lines and wide open areas are difficult for him. Additionally, his knockback is quite far so maps with nearby ledges give him the chance for environmental kills.

## How to Counter Doomfist 

To counter ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist, crowd control is practically required. The way to play around Doom without stuns is if you have the mobility to constantly avoid him. Considering his mobility is among the best in the game, this is easier said than done. 

Never shoot him while he's blocking unless he is currently under roughly 20% HP, or has no Supports nearby that can heal him. Simply not giving him empowered punch is the best way to survive his attacks.

The easiest time to stun him is during his block. If he starts to bait this by canceling his block into slam and dodging your moves, it's a mind game to see who will waste their abilities.

The other best time is while he channels ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rocket-punch.png)Rocket Punch, or immediately after he uses ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/seismic-slam.png)Seismic Slam since you know he won't be able to suddenly move unless he uses his ultimate.
