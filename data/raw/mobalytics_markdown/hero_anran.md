# Overwatch Overwatch Anran Guide and Overview

In this Overwatch Anran guide, you'll learn the fundamentals of their abilities, unique play styles, and fundamental tips. Master ability combos, positioning, and matchup knowledge to dominate with Anran!

## Anran Overview

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran is a Dive-oriented DPS who uses a combination of burst damage and damage over time attacks. Her kit is very combo-heavy, as none of her abilities are designed to be used in isolation from the rest of her kit. 

Mobility and ability combos are the tools ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran has at her disposal to outplay the enemy. She has good damage potential, but needs to be at medium-close range, where she can attack with her secondary fan and fade. Off angles and flanks are where she should be spending most of her time. Similar to other flank DPS heroes, she finds the most success by using her mobility to bypass the enemies' frontline and isolate the targets that are behind the rest of their team.

If you enjoy heroes with high skill expression, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran is an excellent choice going into the brand new Season 1 of Overwatch.

### TLDR

#### Playing As

* Alternate between 2 Primary Fire > 1 Secondary Fire, to maximize DPS.
* Only use one ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/dancing-blaze.png)Dancing Blaze dash to attack, and always save one charge to escape fights and survive.
* Both Ults are valuable. Although it’s better to secure elims with Ascent, bringing yourself immediately back into the fight and helping your team win is a great use of the revive ult.

#### Playing Against

* Heroes with the mobility and range to stay outside of the 10m range of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fan-the-flames.png)Fan the Flames have an advantage in the match-up.
* Crowd Control and stuns are very effective for shutting down ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran's rush. Target her and finish her off when she tries to escape.
* Quickly target ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran during her ult. If she’s at low HP or takes a second to aim, you can eliminate her while she’s channeling ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/vermillion-ascent.png)Vermillion Ascent.

#### Strengths

* High survivability thanks to mobility, dodge, and self-healing abilities.
* Good combination of damage over time from burning, and burst damage from combos.

#### Weaknesses

* Needs to stay within the short range of her secondary fire to maintain high DPS output. Many heroes have the tools to make this a very difficult task.
* Mechanically challenging. Requires the capability to quickly chain ability combos, track burning duration, and control quick rush speed.

## Strengths And Weaknesses Explained

### Strengths

#### High survivability

High survivability thanks to mobility, dodge, and self-healing abilities. Although she has to play at closer ranges, her kit comes with plenty of tools to stay alive.

#### Good DoT and burst

Good combination of damage over time from burning, and burst damage from combos. While many heroes have one or the other, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran has multiple methods of getting eliminations.

### Weaknesses

#### Short ranged

Needs to stay within the short range of her secondary fire to maintain high DPS output. Many heroes have the tools to make this a very difficult task. Against other highly mobile heroes, it can be difficult to chase. When the enemy is running longer ranged hitscans, getting into position is dangerous.

#### Difficult to play

Mechanically challenging. Requires the capability to quickly chain ability combos, track burning duration, and control quick rush speed.

#### Weak to Crowd Control

Stuns are especially good at shutting her down. Other than a perfectly timed![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/dancing-blaze.png)Dancing Blaze, she's completely vulnerable to getting chain-stunned to death.

## Anran Ability Tips

### Primary Fire: Zhuque Fans

  
**Tips for** Zhuque Fans  

* Best ranged damage tool. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/zhuque-fans.png)Zhuque Fans have no falloff damage and igniting some at range is a solid method of building ult charge.
* Even when you are trying to set up a flank, you should still be trying to get in as much poke damage as possible while repositioning.

### Secondary Fire: Fan the Flames

  
**Tips for** Fan the Flames  

* Short range and low base DPS. Only use to ignite, or to keep enemies ignited.
* Deals 25% ignition, same as primary fire body shots. When the enemy ignition UI is at 75%, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fan-the-flames.png)Fan the Flames will simultaneously ignite and trigger amplified burning damage.
* You can HOLD primary fire, and tap secondary fire to animation cancel the recovery of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/zhuque-fans.png)Zhuque Fans and min/max your damage output.

### \[SHIFT\]: Inferno Rush

  
**Tips for** Inferno Rush  

* Treat Rush like a dash or jump. The initial boost can be aimed vertically, but during the rush, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran cannot gain additional height.
* * ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/inferno-rush.png)Inferno Rush is her best escape tool. When near high ground or walls, jump+SHIFT while aiming up to quickly boost yourself over the wall to safety.
* One charge for engage, one for disengage. Since you have two boosts available and want to be within Fan’s range, your go-to combat loop should be to use the first charge to pounce the enemy. Try to get the KO or at least get their team to use multiple cooldowns, then use your E to buy time, and SHIFT to get out.
* * Never use both dashes offensively unless your entire team is grouped and committed to fighting the same target as you.

### \[E\]: Dancing Blaze 

  
**Tips for** Dancing Blaze  

* Requires precise timing. The base duration is less than 1 second, so you’ll need to be very precise with your activation timing.
* * Try not to preemptively activate ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/dancing-blaze.png)Dancing Blaze. Instead, watch the opponent and react to their ability animations to dodge their attack.
* Slight movement control during the dodge. Even though it’s not much, moving to a different spot during the invisibility is very important to make it difficult for the enemy to instantly attack you upon reappearing.
* Best in crowds. If you are within range of multiple opponents, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran will continue attacking each target once, resulting in her staying invulnerable for longer.
* * However, this does not increase her movement range.

### Ultimate: Vermillion Ascent and Vermillion Revival

  
**Tips for** Vermillion Ascent  

* Not invincible. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran can be killed while channeling ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/vermillion-ascent.png)Vermillion Ascent. Make sure you have enough health (\~175HP+) to survive long enough to aim and charge forward.
* Huge AoE. Check the visual indicator to verify which enemies will be in the explosion radius.
* * Although there is falloff damage from the center of impact, all enemies hit will be 100% ignited.  
   * Always follow up your Ult with ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fan-the-flames.png)Fan the Flames to quickly amplify burning damage.
* The dive goes pierces through barriers, but the actual explosion can be blocked. Make sure you wait out barrier abilities before activating her Ult.
* Combo tool.
* * Think of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/vermillion-ascent.png)Vermillion Ascent as just one part of a combo. You can either start the combo with it, or use it mid-combo. The dive is not an instant-win or instakill button. To get high value, you’ll need to quickly land primary and secondary fires along with the Ult.

**Tips For** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/vermillion-revival.png)Vermillion Revival  

* If you die near enemies, wait until your team pushes up to create a safe revival space for you. When they are unable to, save your Ult charge for your next life.
* Not a ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/d-va.png)D.Va Bomb. Although ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran explodes upon revival, the burst damage is very low. Be very cautious when reviving, as you’ll need to quickly evade or burst down nearby targets with your secondary and primary fire.
* * Use as much of the allowed.

## Perks

### Minor Perks

Smoulder  

* Extends the time of burning.
* Does **not** extend ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fan-the-flames.png)Fan the Flames amplified damage time.
* Useful for extending damage on enemies that retreat to cover. Will sometimes score an elim where the enemy could’ve just barely lived otherwise.
* Further delays the self-healing passive from activating.

Heat Shield  

* Turns ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/vermillion-ascent.png)Vermillion Ascent into a survival tool. The large AoE makes it consistent and realistic to expect to hit multiple targets per use.
* Enables more aggressive use of your ult. Hitting 2 targets, for example, generates 150 overhealth.
* Use the overhealth generation mid-fight to as a form of healing, or at the start of an attack to make yourself more tanky.
* Recommended Minor Perk.

### Major Perks

#### Short Fuse  

* Rushing through multiple targets in a scrambly fight is a great way to spread burning, inflict damage, and evade damage.
* Hitting the same target twice counts as double cooldown reduction (3s total).
* Be smart about where you position yourself when the Rush ends. You’d have to get 6 impacts to fully refresh the Rush if you don’t have either charge up.

#### Hungering Blaze  

* Extremely potent survival ability.
* Being in range of multiple enemies is even more important when using Hungering Blaze. As mentioned under Ability Tips, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran will stay invulnerable for longer and additionally heal herself more.
* Recommended Major Perk.

## Maps

On _Control_ and _Flashpoint_maps (e.g., Nepal, Lijiang Tower, Suravasa), ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran thrives by leveraging verticality and flank routes to harass isolated enemies and secure early picks. She needs to use a get-in-get out strategy on these smaller maps or else the enemy team can peel for her target, then focus fire her to a quick death.

On _Escort_, _Hybrid,_ and _Push_ maps (e.g., New Queen Street, Dorado, Colosseo), use her mobility to similarly threaten Supports and DPS set up in the backlines of their team. The more linear layout of these maps merits longer poke phases compared to the two previous modes.

Anran’s kit rewards players who can read team rotations, abuse environmental cover to be difficult to predict or eliminate, and punish enemies whose anti-dive abilites are on cooldown (E.G., ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/sleep-dart.png)Sleep Dart, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/flashbang.png)Flashbang, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fade.png)Fade).

Because ranged combat is her primary weakness, on maps with long sightlines like Midtown and Circuit Royal, she gets outclassed by hitscans like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ashe.png)Ashe.

## Team Comp Synergies

The best Tanks to have on your team are able to use damage mitigation to help you stay alive, or can follow up on your engages. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/zarya.png)Zarya can give ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/projected-barrier.png)Projected Barrier to allow Anran to slip behind enemies, and safely attack without being CC'd. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/d-va.png)D.Va can fly in with ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/defense-matrix.png)Defense Matrix and her burst damage for a coordinated dive assassination. 

Since ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran lacks consistent pressure from her damage, she can also works well with heroes such as ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/soldier-76.png)Soldier: 76 or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/cassidy.png)Cassidy who can constantly apply poke pressure and can follow up on Anran's dive from range.

## How to Counter Anran

Crowd control such as slows, stuns, or displacements are the best ways to coutner ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/anran.png)Anran during her attacks. Kocking her back is good to disrupt her attack, or get out of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fan-the-flames.png)Fan the Flames range. If you can stun her out of her dash or immediately when she reappears after ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/dancing-blaze.png)Dancing Blaze, you should be able to get the elimination.

Movement and the use of cover to prevent her from repeatedly landing hits makes it difficult for her to keep up burning damage on you. Once her burn gauge resets, she struggles to re-establish pressure.

High-burst DPS such as ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sojourn.png)Sojourn, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/cassidy.png)Cassidy, or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/vendetta.png)Vendetta can intercept Anran before she can be effectice. They also excel at finishing her off during ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/inferno-rush.png)Inferno Rush or after ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/dancing-blaze.png)Dancing Blaze.

Don't forget to keep an eye on her death location when you hear her use ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/vermillion-revival.png)Vermillion Revival, becuase she can be an easy kill if she's located away from her team.
