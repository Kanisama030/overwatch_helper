# Overwatch Overwatch Sigma Guide and Overview

In this Overwatch Sigma guide, you'll learn the fundamentals of their abilities, unique play styles, and fundamental tips. Master ability combos, positioning, and matchup knowledge to excel with other Overwatch [heroes](https://mobalytics.gg/overwatch/heroes/sigma-guide/overwatch/heroes)!

## Sigma Overview

With the ability to control gravity, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma crushes every enemy in his path. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma's power to control the tempo and positioning of team fights is nearly unmatched in the ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/tank.svg)Tank category. Being able to simultaneously mitigate enemy projectiles while dealing tons of his own damage has made ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma consistent across different types of metas. Unless he is nerfed into the ground, there are never scenarios where ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma feels useless. 

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma's AoE poke damage rapidly boosts his ultimate charge and keeps the enemy teams consistently low on HP which gives his team an influx of opportunities for playmaking. The way he can contest space is not by standing directly in the enemy's way, but through dishing out high levels of damage whilst taking very low damage himself. 

The ideal range for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma to play in is made obvious by his primary fire, so he is always looking for ways to rotate and maintain his perfect distance. When facing a team with huge amounts of poke damage, no other tank can deal with a ranged onslaught better than this astrophysicist. 

### TLDR

#### Playing As

* Position at mid-range poke distances to maximize ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres damage while keeping yourself outside of the enemy's optimal range.
* Use ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/experimental-barrier.png)Experimental Barrier to the enemy ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/support.svg)Supports damage and healing from reaching their ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/tank.svg)Tank or your team.
* Track escape abilities like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fade.png)Fade before activating ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/gravitic-flux.png)Gravitic Flux or else the enemy will easily avoid its AoE.

#### Playing Against

* Force ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma off his optimal mid-range poke angles by pressuring him with dive or flank threats, exploiting his lack of mobility.
* Beam heroes are strong against ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma because they can burn through his shield and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kinetic-grasp.png)Kinetic Grasp doesn't absorb beams.
* Stun him out of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/gravitic-flux.png)Gravitic Flux to immediately cancel his ult before he has a chance to activate.

## Strengths And Weaknesses Explained

### Strengths

#### High AoE damage output

At the end of games, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma will often lead the scoreboard with total damage dealt. Especially against teams that play grouped near their tank, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma's direct impact damage on one enemy plus splash damage on multiple enemies quickly racks up. Additionally, the ricochet effect of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres makes ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma extremely powerful at controlling tight spaces like hallways and choke points.

#### Movable shield

There are many shield abilities in OW2 but ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma alone has the ability to constantly redeploy his shield and set it anywhere he pleases. No other shield can be left floating in mid-air, perfectly blocking an enemy hero's LoS. Saving shield health and using it to react to specific ability usages, splitting off a target from the rest of their team or simply soaking up the full 700 HP of damage can all be great uses of his shield. 

#### Survivability

Even when ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma's team has lost heroes during a team fight, it is never over if he is still alive. Excellent use of cover between shots of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres, Barrier and Grasp combine to allow ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma to take very little damage and require little to no resources from his supports to stay alive. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/accretion.png)Accretion also works as a great anti-dive CC tool to shut down enemy movement abilities or give ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma and his allies time to escape.

### Weaknesses

#### No mobility abilities

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma's greatest and most exploitable weakness comes in his complete lack of mobility tools. Other than during his ultimate, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma has no way to speed himself up or move vertically and is technically the only ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/tank.svg)Tank without any way to do so. Positioning is extremely important for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma because he is essentially stuck wherever he is currently standing. The poor mobility also makes him weak against high mobility and dive comps.

#### Difficulty solo confirming elims

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma's highest burst combo (![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/accretion.png)Accretion → ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres → melee) only deals 230 dmg leaving the standard 250 HP hero the chance to live, escape and keep fighting. Even though his ult combo (Flux → ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres → slam) can eliminate a single target, there are multiple abilities that counter ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/gravitic-flux.png)Gravitic Flux and enable enemies to survive the combo. While he has high overall high damage output, the damage tends to be too slow or inconsistent to consistently confirm elims without team assistance. 

#### Slow primary fire projectile speed and fire rate

The speed of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres is slow and requires a good deal of leading your shots in order to land direct hits. The small gap between each one of the two orbs in his double shots is also just large enough to make it difficult to land a direct hit with both orbs. Top all that off with the slow rate of fire and consistently hitting small hurtbox heroes like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/tracer.png)Tracer and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/kiriko.png)Kiriko becomes very challenging for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma players.

## Sigma Ability Tips

### \[Primary Fire\]: Hyperspheres

  
Tips for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres  

* ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma is a front-line Tank that indirectly contests space. (As opposed to a hero like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/reinhardt.png)Reinhardt who stands directly in the spot he is contesting).
* * While shots are easier to land at point-blank range, Sigma typically wants to play near his max range. His slow rate of fire makes jiggle peeking a wall or corner really powerful.  
   * Expose yourself only during the moments Sigma is actually firing ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres then quickly retreat behind cover.
* ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres bounce, so look for opportunities to attack enemies around corners and inside rooms.
* Aim towards the enemies feet for more consistent direct hits. The hurt boxes are larger, and if you miss slightly, sometimes you'll catch them with the bounce.

#### Positioning

* Even when using ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/experimental-barrier.png)Experimental Barrier to position more aggressively, it is best to stay near some type of cover, even if just partial cover like the Payload. The cover still allows Sigma to cycle through use of cover, Barrier and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kinetic-grasp.png)Kinetic Grasp to protect himself from incoming damage and stay alive as long as possible.
* Rotating around an objective is a crucial skill for Sigma players to learn. To do this, focus on maintaining a distance to the enemies that keeps them within your primary fire range, but outside of close and melee range. As the enemies move towards you, circumnavigate the objective area, or pieces of cover in order to keep the distance and keep pressuring them with your AoE damage.

### \[Secondary Fire\]: Experimental Barrier

  
Tips for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/experimental-barrier.png)Experimental Barrier  

* Try to never let the shield break
* * Leaving the shield with even 1 HP will save you the 5 extra seconds of cooldown before beginning to regenerate shield health.  
   * Only need 1 HP of shield available to block big abilities like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/self-destruct.png)Self-Destruct, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/sleep-dart.png)Sleep Dart, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/chain-hook.png)Chain Hook etc.
* Use to block high value projectile abilites rather than burning health on random poke damage.
* * Depending on the enemy comp and situation, the Experimental Barrier can be useful for general damage mitigation but its best use is to decide what heroes have big impact abilities and try to save some shield health to react to them.  
   * In addition to those listed above, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/cardiac-overdrive.png)Cardiac Overdrive, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/jagged-blade.png)Jagged Blade, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/earthshatter.png)Earthshatter, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/biotic-grenade.png)Biotic Grenade are other good examples.
* Play near cover so you can recall shield, and corner peek while shield health regenerates.
* Recall shield before using Kinetic Grasp to maximize shield health management.
* Block snipers LoS such as Widowmaker, Ashe, and Ana who play back from their team and do very little damage to barriers.
* * Great for blocking high-ground positions  
   * When doing this, place the shield behind as many of the enemy heroes as possible so it doesn't break.

### \[Shift\]: Kinetic Grasp

  
Tips for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kinetic-grasp.png)Kinetic Grasp  

* Since Sigma is completely vulnerable to beam damage for the full 2 seconds or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kinetic-grasp.png)Kinetic Grasp, you’ll ideally want to be positioned where the beam heroes can’t attack you when you use Kinetic Grasp.
* The hitbox is actually a large rectangle in front of Sigma and absorbs all projectiles that come in contact with it. The AoE is rather generous and can be treated like a mini ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/defense-matrix.png)Defense Matrix.
* Absorbing damage from turrets like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/deploy-turret.png)Deploy Turret and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/b-o-b.png)B.O.B. guarantees overhealth generation.
* Grasp is countered by non-absorbable stuns, so be careful not to position too aggressively when the enemy has CC abilities like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/earthshatter.png)Earthshatter or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/overrun.png)Overrun available.

### \[E\]: Accretion

  
Tips for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/accretion.png)Accretion  

* Landing a direct hit to knock down and stun an enemy is ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma’s sole counter-option against enemy aggression.
* * Against a flanking DPS, hitting an ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/accretion.png)Accretion usually leads to an elimination assuming other allies are following up.
* When facing most mobility Tanks, you can stop their dives or escapes with Accretion.
* * Knock down ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/winston.png)Winston during his ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/jump-pack.png)Jump Pack, and you'll often be able to get the kill.
* Accretion is a great tool to stop channeled abilities (![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/power-block.png)Power Block ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/block-nemesis-form.png)Block (Nemesis Form), etc.)
* Large hitbox but slow-moving means the ability is best used when enemies are making a predictable movement. (i.e., walking to cover, jumping, falling, movement abilities like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/guardian-angel.png)Guardian Angel or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/violent-leap.png)Violent Leap etc.)

### \[Q\]: (Ultimate) Gravitic Flux

  
Tips for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/gravitic-flux.png)Gravitic Flux  

* There is a 1 second activation delay time once a location is selected before enemies are locked in the ult.
* * * Track enemy escape abilities such as ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/swift-step.png)Swift Step, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fade.png)Fade, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/power-slide.png)Power Slide, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/fortify.png)Fortify. If you didn't just see these types of abilities used, assume the enemy will be able to evade your ult.
* Two direct ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres (one volley) during his ult with eliminate 300 HP heroes like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/mei.png)Mei.
* Use cover
* * Hard knockdowns and stuns will cancel your ult. You can strafe in the air behind cover, activate, hide, then peek to shoot ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres.  
   * Place ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/experimental-barrier.png)Experimental Barrier in front of where you will fly to protect yourself.  
   * If the enemy for sure has a stun like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/sleep-dart.png)Sleep Dart, it's better to just activate and hide until the slam. Let your team shoot them in the air.

## Perks

### Minor Perks

Kinetic Cycle  

* Decent when struggling to maintain distance for dive and brawl ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/tank.svg)Tanks. Otherwise, take the other.

Hyper Regeneration  

* Barrier health is very important, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma deals a lot of damage from his primary fire. Your team gains a significant amount of increased shielding, and in turn, everyone has an easier time staying alive.

### Major Perks

Hyper Strike  

* Interesting ability that is pretty good at countering any enemy with a primarily close-ranged kit. Send them floating and out of their optimal range.

Levitation  

* Very good as it gives ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma the one thing he is missing, mobility.
* Best use is to traverse between high ground areas where your poke pressure can dominate.

## Maps

### Best Maps

#### Circuit Royal

* Watch any pro game and you’ll see ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma is a must pick on Circuit Royal. For us normal folk, he is still the strongest ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/tank.svg)Tank pick for this long range map. Circuit Royal is basically 3 long hallways that ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma's abilities are all perfectly suited for. Barrier cuts off sight lines from enemies, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kinetic-grasp.png)Kinetic Grasp only needs to absorb damage from one direction since flanking on this map is very uncommon. Narrow areas give ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma an easier time landing his primary fire and he can use all the walls to help aim for direct hits as well.

#### Junkertown

* Most of the time you’ll be playing with and facing off against poke heavy team comps on Junkertown. Each of the three points are very open which can make it difficult to constantly land ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres, but ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma's defensive options in these areas with long LoS is unmatched.

#### Midtown

* On Defense ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma can focus on setting up strong position to hold and spam the choke points. On offense, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma can slowly work his way forward as he uses his defensive abilities and takes space for his team to move with him. Most of the defensive positions available to the enemy team keep targets within ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma's range since most fights occur at a mid-range. There aren’t really any areas in the entire map where ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma's lack of mobility hinders his ability to contribute to the fight.

### Worst Maps

#### Watchpoint Gibraltar

* Every point on Gibraltar is loaded with strong high ground areas that ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma cannot easily access and has a difficult time contesting enemies who are.

#### Ilios

* On Well, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma is very vulnerable to being CC’d into the Well and he doesn’t have a reliable means of counterplay. Ruins and Lighthouse both force fights to be at closer quarters than ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma prefers and he has difficulty controlling the spacing.

#### Nepal

* Prepare to spend the entire match trying not to get run over by enemy aggression. Nepal has a few areas ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma can play well in, but he needs to play nearly perfectly the entire game whereas a brawl or mobility tank only needs to make a few correct choices to win the maps against him. Even in Sanctum, where more long range engagements occur, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma tends to crumble as soon as the enemy breaks through his first line of defense.

  
## Team Comp Synergies

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma is best suited for poke team comps. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma is really good at taking up enemy focus and resources which allows long range allies behind him to attack more freely. Shutting down enemy poke heroes is also an area ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma excels in, so the odds are in his teammates' favor. The whole team can work together to chip away at enemy health and resources before the enemy ever has a chance to fully commit to their attack.

### Individual synergies with other heroes

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/cassidy.png)Cassidy

High Noon can be difficult to get kills with and often ends up being a zoning ultimate. Flux at minimum puts its target at 50% HP which is exactly where ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/focusing-beam.png)Focusing Beam does maximum damage and allows ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/echo.png)Echo to constantly confirm kills off of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma's Ultimate.
* ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/echo.png)Echo's poke damage and off-angling pairs really well with how and where ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma wants to play. She can also confrim kills off of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/accretion.png)Accretion knockdowns with Beam.

* ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/baptiste.png)Baptiste's poke damage is one of the best utilities a ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/support.svg)Support can add to ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma's kit. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/amplification-matrix.png)Amplification Matrix's damage boost applies to both ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/accretion.png)Accretion and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/hyperspheres.png)Hyperspheres which makes the two attacks a “one shot” combo against every non-Tank hero in the game. Additionally, the slow movement of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma makes him an easy target for ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/baptiste.png)Baptiste to heal.

  
## How to Counter Sigma 

Any time you are playing against a ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma you want to figure out where he is trying to position, then track which of his abilities best counter yours. If you are playing ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/winston.png)Winston, you don’t need to pay any attention to ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/experimental-barrier.png)Experimental Barrier or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kinetic-grasp.png)Kinetic Grasp, but ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/accretion.png)Accretion can easily mean your death if he hits you out of a ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/jump-pack.png)Jump Pack. 

Regardless of who you are playing, you’ll benefit from constantly repositioning to outplay his Barrier usage. Then focus on closing the distance to him and trying to get him stuck somewhere he is unable to retreat.

#### Specific Hero Counters

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/zarya.png)Zarya  

* ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/zarya.png)Zarya's a beam hero, which means every time ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma uses ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/kinetic-grasp.png)Kinetic Grasp, she can nonstop blast him for two full seconds! When pressing forward into ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/accretion.png)Accretions can easily be reacted to with a ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/particle-barrier.png)Particle Barrier, preventing its CC effect and giving a burst of energy charge to ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/zarya.png)Zarya.

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/symmetra.png)Symmetra  

* First off, her primary fire charges up insanely fast anytime ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/experimental-barrier.png)Experimental Barrier is in front of her. Then, when it's down, the high charge beam melts ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma. Secondly, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/teleporter.png)Teleporter can reposition her entire team in a way ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma has no hope of keeping up with or responding to. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/symmetra.png)Symmetra vs ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma is one of the hardest counters in all of OW2 when played correctly.

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/lucio.png)Lúcio  

* On his own, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/lucio.png)Lúcio can wall ride around and be a nuisance to ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma, who has difficulty tracking the speedy ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/roles/support.svg)Support. The way ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/lucio.png)Lúcio truly counters ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma, however, is by speed boosting his immobile allies into the ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma, who has no way of escaping without an ally's assistance. Many short-range heroes (![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/mei.png)Mei, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/reinhardt.png)Reinhardt, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/symmetra.png)Symmetra, etc) are very strong against ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma but only if they can close the distance on him. ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/lucio.png)Lúcio gives them all a consistent way to do just that.
