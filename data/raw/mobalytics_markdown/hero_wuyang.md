# Overwatch Overwatch Wuyang Guide and Overview

In this Overwatch Wuyang guide, you'll learn the fundamentals of their abilities, unique play styles, and fundamental tips. Master ability combos, positioning, and matchup knowledge to excel with other Overwatch [heroes](https://mobalytics.gg/overwatch/heroes/wuyang-guide/overwatch/heroes)!

## Wuyang Overview

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/wuyang.png)Wuyang is a flexible Support hero who combines sustained healing, burst utility, and long-range damage into a single fluid kit. Trained in ancient water techniques, Wuyang approaches combat like the tide: steady when needed, or a huge wave of damage and healing when the moment is right. His mastery over water allows him to both mend allies and batter enemies, flowing seamlessly between protection and lethality. 

In team compositions, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/wuyang.png)Wuyang functions as a frontline Support who plays closer to the action than most healers. He excels at enabling aggressive pushes, sustaining his Tank throughout the mid-fight, and providing burst healing in critical, fight-determining moments.

### TLDR

#### Play As

* Wait to use your Ult until you or your selected ally is right next to enemies, so it doesn’t expire before getting into attack range.
* Use Overflow to refill Wuyang’s healing resource while healing your Tank’s large health pool.
* Combine Guardian Wave with another Support’s AoE healing, such as Moira’s Biotic Orb.

#### Play Against

* When you see the ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/guardian-wave.png)Guardian Wave slam, back off, wait a few seconds, then hard re-engage to wipe his team because they won’t have burst healing.
* After you see him use Rushing Torrent (often to refill healing resources), immediately rush at him to eliminate Wuyang while he’s unable to escape.
* His Ult has a short 3.5s duration. Run away, use cover, and escape tools your hero has because it’s basically impossible to survive if you get caught.

## Strengths And Weaknesses Explained

### Strengths

#### Versatile Healing Options

Between ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/restorative-stream.png)Restorative Stream’s passive and active modes, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/guardian-wave.png)Guardian Wave’s healing amplification, and Tidal Blast’s burst heal, Wuyang has plenty of options to choose from when teammates are under fire.

Additionally, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/restorative-stream.png)Restorative Stream is one of just a handful of healing abilities that can heal through barriers, giving him the advantage in many scenarios.

#### Consistent Ranged Pressure

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/wuyang.png)Wuyang’s ranged damage is among the best in the Support class. Steering an orb blast to hit around corners is a unique strength that makes him a constant threat. While his damage potential is not quite as high as Zen’s, he offers much higher healing as a trade-off.

#### Fight Winning Ultimate

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/tidal-blast.png)Tidal Blast is one of the best Ults in the entire game. Overhealth, hard knockdown, burst healing all in one. Entire team fights can be won with just a well-timed Wuyang Ult.

### Weaknesses

#### Utility Focused Kit

Although this is technically one of his strengths, it’s also his weakness. To be a worthwhile pick, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/wuyang.png)Wuyang players must be able to manage their resources, read fights, track enemy positions, and know when to use his high value cooldowns. His abilities are really easy to waste and cause your team to lose the game.

#### Tricky Aim Mechanics

All the time you’ve spent clicking heads with hitscans won’t help you much when steering water orbs across the map. To be consistent with curving shots around corners requires a decent amount of time dedicated to this unique mechanic. 

#### Limited Escape Options

Unlike the original iteration of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/wuyang.png)Wuyang, which was way overtuned and nearly impossible to dive, the current version is vulnerable to dive enemies. One of the main ways he keeps his Tank alive is by using ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rushing-torrent.png)Rushing Torrent with Overflow Perk to pump out more single-target healing. Doing this leaves him without an escape tool, so smart enemies will wait and punish him.

## Wuyang Ability Tips

### Primary Fire: Xuanwu Staff

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/xuanwu-staff.png)Xuanwu Staff  

* Water orbs need to travel about 8m before they can become empowered. When you are very close to your target, it is better to spam fire instead of trying to charge and control your shots.
* * Rapid firing at close range deals the same damage per orb, but the fire rate is significantly faster, resulting in much higher damage output.  
   * Empowered water orbs consume 2 ammo.
* Fully charged orbs deal more damage and can be guided. To min/max his damage per second, you can release them as soon as the charge completes and fire another volley. The orbs will slowly drop and spread out, so aim a bit high when doing this.
* Finish off low-health enemies around corners by curving orbs to hit enemies that are hiding out of your LoS.

### Secondary Fire: Restorative Stream

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/restorative-stream.png)Restorative Stream  

* Prioritize squishy allies, and especially those diving or taking up a lot of the enemy's attention.
* Treat ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/restorative-stream.png)Restorative Stream as primarily a passive ability. Set it and forget it. Focus on keeping your damage output high.
* Don’t spam target swapping.
* * Every time you heal with the beam, you trigger another 2-second delay before the healing resource refills itself.
* Remember to use your healing resource. Even though we just said not to get carried away with it, you still need to be very active with keeping allies healthy with your healing beam.
* * See an ally at half HP? Focus heal them.
* Both active and passive healing ignore barriers.
* * Typically, it is ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/wuyang.png)Wuyang's job to sustain aggressive allies pushing past enemy barriers

### \[SHIFT\]: Rushing Torrent

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rushing-torrent.png)Rushing Torrent  

* Grants a burst of speed followed by sustained mobility and increased jump height. Primarily, you should use it proactively to reposition to corners and high ground as your team moves around.
* Against teams with heavy dive or flank comps, always save this mobility tool and use it to escape their attack, or dash to a teammate for help.

### \[E\]: Guardian Wave

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/guardian-wave.png)Guardian Wave  

* Deals damage, heals allies, knocks enemies back, and increases healing received by 50% for 3 seconds.
* Best used once your team has taken a good amount of damage
* Don’t get baited into using it for a tiny bit of poke damage. Hitting an enemy Tank for 80 damage essentially does nothing.
* Good for finishing off enemies in close-range battles, especially if they are very mobile and difficult to hit with your primary fire.

### Ultimate: Tidal Blast

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/tidal-blast.png)Tidal Blast  

* Works best on aggressive, fast heroes diving into the enemy team. Always wait for your ally to be actively attack the enemy before giving them your ult.
* * Ping “Ult is Ready” to signal that you will assist them, so they know to be extra aggressive.
* Also works great as a tide shifter. When the enemy has committed multiple ultimates to try and eliminate your Tank, for example, you can keep them alive with overhealth and empower them to unleash a deadly counterattack.

## Perks

### Minor Perks

#### Overflow  

* Grants the ability to give more burst healing to your allies, and less offensive downtime for yourself due to less time spent reloading.
* Recommended Minor Perk

#### Balance  

* Allows you to focus even more on dealing damage. As long as you keep hitting enemies, your selected ally will keep benefitting from increased healing.

### Major Perks

#### Ebb and Flow  

* Not a terrible perk, but not worth taking over Falling Rain.
* May help with countering dive comps by improving your close-range capabilities.
* Try it out if you have been unable to get value out of your charged primary fire up to this point.

#### Falling Rain  

* Very powerful for increasing total and burst damage.
* As soon as you unlock the Major Perk, you should start putting more emphasis on dealing damage.
* All the same tips from ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/xuanwu-staff.png)Xuanwu Staff apply as the use case does not change.

## Maps

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/wuyang.png)Wuyang has good horizontal and vertical mobility, long-range damage, and medium-range healing. These attributes allow him to adapt to most maps.

Regardless of the map, he wants to take advantage of his curving projectile to hit around corners, and jiggle peek cover to minimize his exposure to damage.

The maps he struggles most on are those with high ground areas that are outside his jump reach. Watchpoint: Gibraltar and Numbani, for example, have objective areas surrounded by high ground that Wuyang can't reach to contest or follow his team.

## Team Comp Synergies

As a versatile Support, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/wuyang.png)Wuyang can work with rush, dive, and poke team comps. He just needs to adapt his playstyle to fit his team. For example, on a dive team, make sure you have ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/restorative-stream.png)Restorative Stream placed on the teammate playing most aggressively and taking up the enemy team's attention. To do this, you generally need to use ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rushing-torrent.png)Rushing Torrent to keep up with their movement and stay within healing range.

On the other hand, when playing on a poke-heavy team, you should primarily be saving ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rushing-torrent.png)Rushing Torrent as an escape tool to get away from enemy attacks, since you won't need it to keep up with your team's more stationary playstyle.

For Supports, try to play with a hero who has AoE healing like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/mizuki.png)Mizuki or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/lucio.png)Lúcio to make up for your single-target healing. Also, your team will get more benefit from ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/guardian-wave.png)Guardian Wave's boosted healing when you hit multiple allies, and they are all being healed simultaneously by your other Support.

Just remember that if you are paired with an ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ana.png)Ana, healing boosts from ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/biotic-grenade.png)Biotic Grenadeand ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/guardian-wave.png)Guardian Wave do not stack. These are two extremely high-value cooldowns, and your team can't afford to waste them by layering them together.

## How to Counter Wuyang 

The main way to beat ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/wuyang.png)Wuyang is by tracking his ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/guardian-wave.png)Guardian Wave and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/rushing-torrent.png)Rushing Torrent cooldowns. When he has these still available, he and his allies are very difficult to eliminate. 

Slowly pressure him and save your most lethal cooldowns until after you've seen him use one or both of these CDs. 

When you see the ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/guardian-wave.png)Guardian Wave slam, back off, wait 3 seconds, then hard re-engage to wipe his team because they won’t have burst healing. After Wave, you know he can't knock you back, and can't rapidly heal his allies. 

After his dash, he doesn't have any way to escape your attack. After you see him use Rushing Torrent (often to refill healing resources), immediately rush at him to eliminate Wuyang while he’s unable to escape.

His Ult has a short 3.5s duration. Run away, use cover, and escape tools your hero has because you don't want to get knocked down by the explosion. 

When you are unable to escape, hard focus the enemy in ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/tidal-blast.png)Tidal Blast. This is less optimal since they'll likely have plenty of health plus the huge chunk of overhealth from the ult.
