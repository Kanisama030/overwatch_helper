# Overwatch Overwatch Freja Guide and Overview

In this Overwatch Freja guide, you'll learn the fundamentals of their abilities, unique play styles, and fundamental tips. Master ability combos, positioning, and matchup knowledge to excel with other Overwatch [heroes](https://mobalytics.gg/overwatch/heroes/freja-guide/overwatch/heroes)!

## Freja Overview

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/freja.png)Freja is a hybrid sniper hero who excels in poke battles and quick bursts of aggression. She likes to position either behind her frontline Tank or take a soft flank angle. Especially when there is high ground nearby the objective area, Freja looks to use Updraft to take the superior position on the enemy team. 

She can hold her own in close-range battles as well, thanks to the short cooldown on her ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/quick-dash.png)Quick Dash. When your team needs a combination of poke pressure and burst damage, Freja is one of the best DPS choices for the job.

### TLDR Freja Guide

#### Play As

* Use Updraft to leap over walls and barriers, allowing you to attack enemies the rest of your team can't reach.
* Save at least one Quick Dash charge to dodge enemy flanks and dives.
* Focus on landing direct hits with Bola Shot to gain the Hindered and pull-in effects.

#### Play Against

* Freja excels at focusing down one target, but is weak when there are multiple enemies. Position near your allies so you don’t end up in a 1v1 situation.
* If you see her use Updraft, know you have a 10-second window where she can’t target you behind cover or run away to high ground (unless she's using Rising Winds).
* Counter Freja by outranging her with a hitscan sniper, or overwhelming her with a close-range, high mobility hero.

## Strengths And Weaknesses Explained

### Strengths

#### High Mobility

Up to three charges of Quick Dash, vertical leap, and the float effect from Take Aim give Freja some of the best in-class mobility. The ability to dash, float, and leap synergizes well with her crossbow kit and allows her to take side angles, shoot over barriers, and disengage quickly.

#### Burst Damage

The explosive bolt from Take Aim deals 130 damage on a direct hit, and even without perks, Freja can fire three in a row with Dash refreshes. While she won’t always have the most consistent damage, when it matters most, she can burst down a single enemy very quickly. 

#### Debuff Abilities

Her Minor Perk, Frost Bolts, applies a 20% slow debuff on targets and greatly helps to even the odds against high mobility enemies. Moreover, while it's not explicitly stated in the ability description, Bola Shot direct hits apply Hindered debuff to the enemy. Freja can use her ult to movement lock or cancel channeled abilities of an enemy and turn the tide of a fight in her team's favor.

### Weaknesses

#### Travel Projectile Sniper

Freja’s kit is best suited for medium to long-range combat. Landing shots with her primary and secondary fire at these longer ranges becomes more difficult because of the travel speed. She shares similar weaknesses to Hanzo, but trades the ability to one-shot for more consistent damage. 

#### Situational Ultimate

Unlike many of the other DPS heroes, Freja’s ultimate isn’t typically going to win fights on its own. Bola Shot has good situational power and sets up her teammates for follow-up on the CC, but most often, she’s just hoping to get a single pick with each use.

#### Multiple ways to counter

Freja has weaknesses on both ends of the ranged spectrum. Close-range mobility heroes and long-range hitscans have favorable matchups against her. Whereas most heroes only have one easily exploitable range weakness, Freja gets countered by both types of heroes, so expect to always be playing into multiple counters. 

## Freja Ability Tips

### Primary Fire: Revdraw Crossbow

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/revdraw-crossbow.png)Revdraw Crossbow  

* Max spread doesn’t kick in until the 12th shot in a row.
* * Even when using Relentless Barrage, you can hold down primary fire until after the 3rd Take Aim bolt before you need to pause and reset her bullet spread.
* Leading shots
* * Use Revdraw bolts to lead shots and measure how much you need to lead the explosive bolts.

### Secondary Fire: Take Aim

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/take-aim-freja.png)Take Aim (Freja)  

* 1 Explosive, 6 standard bolts
* * Fire Take Aim explosive bolt, then spam 6 primary fires, 1 explosive bolt, then repeat to maximize uptime.
* Float Effect
* * Pausing in the air can be great for firing over walls and barriers, while also giving you time to line up your shot. Just be careful not to overuse the float and become predictable, because Freja is very easy to kill while floating.

### \[SHIFT\]: Quick Dash

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/quick-dash.png)Quick Dash  

* Spam explosive bolts
* * The best way for Freja to confirm single target elims is by using Quick Dash to chase down an enemy and fire multiple explosive bolts at them.  
   * If your target is not receiving any healing, two direct hits will eliminate a standard 250HP hero.
* Dodging
* * When facing any dive enemies targeting you, be sure to save at least one Quick Dash to dodge their attack and get in an extra explosive bolt.  
   * If Freja gets caught with no Dashes, she’s as good as dead.

### \[E\]: Updraft

  
**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/updraft.png)Updraft  

* Take high ground
* * Before fights or at the beginning of fights, look to use Updraft to set up on high ground. Freja’s range becomes even stronger when she can control overlooking positions.
* Jump to shoot over barriers
* * Whether it's a Tank deployable barrier or a wall, the best use of Updraft is to leap above cover and target enemies who think they are positioned outside of danger.  
   * Pressuring the backline with a couple of quick explosive bolts can force them to retreat and give up supporting their team.

### Ultimate: Bola Shot

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/bola-shot.png)Bola Shot  

Always go for direct hits

* Even though the AoE damage can be useful on a group of enemies, you should always focus on landing the direct hit. Both the Hindered and pull effect only activate on direct hits.

Prevent the enemy from disengaging

* Dive heroes who rely on mobility tools to get in and get out, and are perfect targets for Bola Shot. Tanks like Winston, Doomfist, and Wrecking Ball can easily be eliminated if you save Bola Shot for just before they try to jump away from your team.

Set up combos

* The grav-like pull effect of a direct impact can be a powerful combo setup. Whether the follow-up is an ally's ultimate or standard ability, it makes for easy wins. Unlike Graviton Surge, the pull effect is too short to wait and react to, so you’ll have to call it out and go on the assumption that your shot will land.

### Passive: Bounty Hunting

**Tips for** ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/bounty-hunting.png)Bounty Hunting  

* Final Blow stocks carry over through death
* * You don’t need to panic and try to get the 3rd final blow before dying. Take your time, and don’t unnecessarily risk your life attempting to get the final blow.
* Ult charge bonus stores
* * The bonus charge will be stored if Freja already has ult. If you are at 3/3 as indicated to the right of your ultimate charge, Freja will immediately gain 25% upon using her ult.  
   * Moreover, if she lands a final blow with her ult, those will count towards the next bonus, so it’s possible to immediately be at 50% ult charge if you get a triple kill.

## Perks

### Minor Perks

Relentless Barrage  

* Recommended Minor Perk
* Significantly increases spam uptime. If you hit multiple direct hits in a row using the standard combo listed under ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/take-aim-freja.png)Take Aim (Freja) tips (6 primary fire > 1 Take Aim > repeat), you would have infinite ammo and never stop to reload.

Momentum Boost  

* Good use is as a counter pick against dive and melee heroes. The extra dash distance can give you the space needed to evade their attacks.
* Activating ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/take-aim-freja.png)Take Aim (Freja) mid-air still freezes Freja, which minimizes the benefit of this Perk.

### Major Perks

Aerial Recovery  

* Recommended Minor Perk.
* Every time you jump and use ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/updraft.png)Updraft \+ ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/take-aim-freja.png)Take Aim (Freja) slow, you'll heal for over 75 HP. Even more when used off a ledge.

Rising Winds  

* Considering the long cooldown of ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/updraft.png)Updraft, doubling its uptime is a considerable buff.
* * Best when playing maps with walls to jump and shoot over.
* Also good as a counterpick against melee and very close-range heroes

## Maps

The best maps for Freja complement her medium-long range positioning. She struggles one map areas with tight spaces and a lot of flat ground. Finding longer sight lines and high ground to set up on is the way Freja primarily wants to position on any map.

### Best Maps

* Dorado
* Blizzard World
* Colosseo

### Worst Maps

* New Junk City
* Nepal
* Oasis

## Team Comp Synergies

![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/freja.png)Freja It is a hybrid character with short to long-range capabilities and good mobility. This allows her to adapt to most team comps. 

Ideally, she has a strong frontline presence from a Tank like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/sigma.png)Sigma or ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/domina.png)Domina, so she can position behind them and spam. These Tanks are also difficult for the enemy to bypass, so ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/freja.png)Freja doesn't have to worry too much about getting rushed down.

For Supports, her favorite is a ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/mercy.png)Mercy pocket, but she works great with pretty much every Support. When using Aerial Recovery, she's less reliant on team sustain so she gets a lot of benefit from Support ulitility like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/biotic-grenade.png)Biotic Grenade, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/orb-of-discord.png)Orb of Discord, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/binding-chain.png)Binding Chain.

## How to Counter Freja 

One way to counter ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/freja.png)Freja is to have better mobility than her, and be able to chase her down and take her out at close range. Heroes like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/d-va.png)D.Va ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/doomfist.png)Doomfist, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/genji.png)Genji, and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/lucio.png)Lúcio can pull this off.

Another way is to outsnipe her. She is in the air often so heroes like ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/widowmaker.png)Widowmaker, ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/ashe.png)Ashe and ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/heroes/zenyatta.png)Zenyatta can delete her if they are ready and waiting for a ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/updraft.png)Updraft \> ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/take-aim-freja.png)Take Aim (Freja).

If your hero doesn't fall into either of these categories, remember that Freja excels at focusing down one target, but is weak when there are multiple enemies. Position near your allies so you don’t end up in a 1v1 situation.

Also, when you see her use Updraft, know you have a 10-second window where she can’t target you behind cover or run away to high ground (unless she's using Rising Winds).  
  
Those with damage mitigation should look to block ![](https://cdn.mobalytics.gg/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto,width=96/assets/overwatch/images/abilities/bola-shot.png)Bola Shot when the hear the ult voice line.
